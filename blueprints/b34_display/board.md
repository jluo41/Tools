## Topic

figure, diagram, illustration, tex, 131 to 346 lines each), a unit contract (`ref/display-unit-output-contract.md`) and
an intake contract. 335 units exist (s01's count: Physician 208, DrFirst 102, WellDoc 24, REACH 1; 107 of them archived) in three
places, plus a fourth under work Runs: Page
Result payloads (`results/<run>/payload/<unit>/`), older `display/` lanes, and built copies under a paper's
`delivery/latex/displays/`. The Excalidraw drawing skills moved out to `1_base/excalidraw/` the same day.

What a display wants, proposed: (1) one unit per figure or table, reproducible from its recipe and data; (2) `built_on`
evidence cards, so its numbers come from the same Runs as the sentence that cites it, and a check that they agree;
(3) one home, the Task's `display/`, with delivery only a built copy; (4) a person's `accepted:` tick, never a score;
(5) the sentence that points to it boxed in the text, its card on the canvas; (6) a missing display flagged as a
proposal, as evidence does.

Excluded: what backs a sentence (b33_evidence); how a sentence is written or judged (b32_draft); Studio drawings
(1_base/excalidraw, b31_excalidraw).
