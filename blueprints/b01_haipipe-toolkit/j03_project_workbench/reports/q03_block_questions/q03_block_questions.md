## Opening

Proposed, not yet answered (261007). Every level, Block, Job and Task, keeps its Questions the
same way: the register in its face (`board.md`, or the Job's or Task's `.md`), one folder per
Question in `reports/qNN_<topic>/`, and its thinking in `studio/sNN-<topic>/`. The studio makes,
the report shows: a topic holds many rough drawings a person edits; a report holds one drawing,
generated from named studio frames listed under its `## Figures`. A topic's sessions are passes
of its own soft Run, `runs/run-draw-<sNN>/passes/pNN-<MMDD>/`. Open: the checker that sees this
shape in every Theme (the acceptance), and whether each Theme's Block follows it today
(studio topic s04, decisions D01 to D06).

**Where this Page sits:** [Q03 · How does every Theme keep its Questions at Block level (the board.md register, reports/qNN_<topic>/, studio/), and how do its Jobs, Tasks and Runs feed those reports?](../../board.md).
