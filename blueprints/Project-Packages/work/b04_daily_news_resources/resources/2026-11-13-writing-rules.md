## What the source says

Gangloff collects recurring academic-writing corrections into a reusable plugin.
Its core covers reader context, terminology, evidence, editing scope and style;
specialist references are read as needed. A separate review procedure checks
paragraph clarity, whole-paper consistency, length, appendices and final polish.
The simulated reader sees only the manuscript through the current paragraph.

## How this could help

The [Draft blueprint](../../../../Project-PageParts/work/b32_draft/board.md)
already owns sentence logic and independent review; the
[Paper blueprint](../../../../Project-Ladder/work/b16_theme_paper/board.md)
owns their use across paper Sections.

| Candidate | Existing practice | Proposed application |
|---|---|---|
| Reader restricted to text seen so far | Sentence-logic review checks introductions and connections. | Trial a separate reader with no chat, code or future paragraphs, to expose assumptions hidden by the editor's knowledge. |
| Durable corrections | Writing and review already have shared rules and reference files. | Record the triggering passage, correction, rationale and scope before promoting repeated feedback into a shared rule. |
| Whole-paper consistency | Local revisions preserve terms, evidence and scope. | Include captions and appendices when checking that a requested terminology change was applied everywhere. |

Relevant owners:
[draft writing](../../../../../plugins/haipipe-toolkit/skills/1_base/draft/haipipe-draft-writing/SKILL.md)
and [draft review](../../../../../plugins/haipipe-toolkit/skills/1_base/draft/haipipe-draft-review/SKILL.md).
The existing review reference already requires introduction before use, stable
terms and real sentence connections. Existing writing guidance rejects a word
blacklist. Keep contextual judgement for useful contrasts and preserve factual
qualifiers; a linter match does not prove poor prose or AI authorship.
