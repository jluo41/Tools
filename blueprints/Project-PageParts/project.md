# Project-PageParts

Decide how any page is made, from its drawings, draft and evidence to its figures and tables, so every paper and report page is built the same way.

One of the three Projects of `Tools/blueprints/` (JL 261028: blueprints holds Projects, as `examples-*/` does in a SPACE; each Project keeps its Blocks in `work/`). Block numbers are unique across all of Tools.

## Blocks

- `work/b31_excalidraw`: How the toolkit's Excalidraw skills draw: the steps from a source file to a drawing, the script behind each step, and what each step costs.
- `work/b32_draft`: What a draft is in every theme: its logic (the plan), its sentences and its evidence, kept in one Page Folder with a draft/ subfolder to work in; whether the Page skills become the Draft skills, and whether page/ and writing/ become one 1_base/draft/ folder.
- `work/b33_evidence`: What backs each sentence in any draft, report or slide, and how it is found: CITE cards from discovery Runs (external) and WORK cards from work Runs (internal), each a topic holding items tied to their sentences and anchor words, read from the Runs on every build, and missing evidence turned into flagged proposals a person ticks.
- `work/b35_studio`: What the Studio is in every Theme: one sNN, one idea, one delivery; what an sNN covers and hands over, how it lives, what it shows on each rung, its mind map, slides and drawings. Moved here from Project-Ladder (b18_theme_studio) on 261030: a page part, not a Theme.
- `work/b34_display`: What a figure or table is in any draft, report or slide, and how it is made: one unit per display holding its recipe, the data it reads, its render and caption, built on evidence cards (CITE or WORK) so its numbers and the sentence citing it cannot disagree, kept in the Task display/ folder, accepted by a person, and copied into delivery only when built.
