# One leaf, one drawing

The rule as built first, then the two loose ends: which leaves get a drawing, and what a mark on one does.

- [workflow] The total workflow <!-- n01 -->
  → Does every leaf need a drawing?
  - [agreed] The map starts with "The total workflow". <!-- n02 -->
  - [agreed] A first-level branch is a section; leaves sit under it. <!-- n03 -->
  - [agreed] Each leaf has one drawing, holding its slides. <!-- n04 -->
  - [agreed] A ✎ link on the leaf opens it in the pop-out. <!-- n05 -->
  - [agreed] The map wraps to the page's width. <!-- n06 -->
- [seed] Does every leaf need its own drawing? <!-- n07 -->
  → What does a red mark do?
  - [open] No empty drawings on disk? <!-- n08 -->

  - [option] Only a leaf with a slide. <!-- n10 -->
  - [leaning] Made the first time a person clicks ✎ draw. <!-- n11 -->
