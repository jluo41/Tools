## Who says a topic is agreed?
- Only JL: one owner for every topic
- The person the topic is for, named on its face
- Anyone, as long as the pass records who said it
leaning: the second (proposal 261029): the face names that person, the pass records the word
ask: Does a topic need an Owner line on its face?

## What does a closed topic leave behind?
- Kept, folded: the folder stays, the page shows it greyed
- Moved to `studio/_closed/`
- Deleted: its answer lives in Report and `delivery/`
leaning: kept, folded (proposal 261029): Links from other topics still land, its Decided lines stay citable
ask: Is a closed topic ever deleted?
