# How does a topic live, from a chat to closed?

The whole life first, then the two steps not settled.

- [workflow] The total workflow <!-- n01 -->
  → Who may say agreed?
  - [agreed] A chat on a rung makes the topic, hung on that rung. <!-- n02 -->
  - [agreed] Each later chat is a pass; Decided and result/ grow. <!-- n03 -->
  - [agreed] explore → shaping → agreed → closed. <!-- n04 -->
  - [agreed] The agreed version is copied to delivery/, then it closes. <!-- n05 -->
- [who] Who says a topic is agreed? <!-- n06 -->
  → What does a closed topic leave?
  - [open] Only JL, or the person it is for? <!-- n07 -->
  - [option] Only JL. <!-- n08 -->
  - [leaning] The person the topic is for, named on its face. <!-- n09 -->
  - [option] Anyone, if the pass records who. <!-- n10 -->
- [close] What does a closed topic leave behind? <!-- n11 -->
  - [open] Is a closed topic ever deleted? <!-- n12 -->
  - [leaning] Kept, folded: Links still land. <!-- n13 -->
  - [option] Moved to studio/_closed/. <!-- n14 -->
  - [option] Deleted; the answer lives in Report and delivery/. <!-- n15 -->
