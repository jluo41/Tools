Decided
-------

s02-D01 · One `studio/` per Block, one sNN numbering; each topic names the rung it hangs from in its face, `**At:** j01` (b17, j01 or j01.t03) (JL 261029: "in this way, sNN should be bNN sNN, or tNN sNN"). Replaced by s02-D08 on 261030.
s02-D02 · A topic's address is `bNN.jNN-sNN` (dots for the ladder, a hyphen before the topic, e.g. `b17.j01-s07`); the folder stays `sNN-<topic>/` (JL 261029: "it should be b17.j01-s07").
s02-D03 · Never write an address alone: the address, a colon, each rung's short name (folder name without its number, first three words) joined by dots, then ` @ ` and the topic, `-` inside a name as `_`: `b17-s04:theme_work @ workbench_page_structure`, `b17.j01.t03-s09:theme_work.tables.cut @ which_cut_first` (JL 261029: "nvery just give the bNNjNN index"; "let's do b17-s04:theme_work @ workbench_page_structure").
s02-D04 · The main things are four: Block `bNN`, Job `jNN`, Task `tNN` and Studio topic `sNN`; a Run `rNN` sits inside its Task. An open topic (explore or shaping) is a decision a person owes; Work rows say how many, "N open topics waiting on you" (JL 261029: "so we just have the bNN jNN tNN and sNN, they are the main things"; "more sNN, means for that item, more human to intervene").
s02-D05 · A Studio topic is where we go round with a person on one thing (an open question, a figure, a deck) until it is good: each round is a pass, "agreed" is the person saying it is good, and the agreed version leaves for `delivery/`. A Task's figure gets a topic at that Task, `**At:** j01.t03` (JL 261029: "一个 task 他画图放到 studio 因为我们是要给这个人说话嘛 ... 我们要看这个图弄得好不好 然后就要不停地改").
s02-D06 · A topic has its own `result/`: what we made while thinking (mock pages, trial pictures, extra drawings and their builders), messy is fine; the top of the topic stays small. A Task Run's output stays in the Run's `result/` and the topic only shows it (JL 261029: "studio 应该也要有 results").
s02-D07 · `delivery/` gets only the clean version, and only after rounds in the Studio: a Task's result goes round with the person, and the agreed version is copied out with its version number (JL 261029: "delivery 可能是从 task 的 results 来 ... 肯定是在我们 studio 腻歪几轮之后，才能够到 delivery").

s02-D08 · No `studio/` folder: each sNN sits directly beside the Jobs or Tasks of the level that uses its delivery (a Block's beside its `jNN_`, a Job's beside its `tNN_`, a Task's beside `runs/`), one sNN numbering per Block, and at most 9 at a Block, 5 at a Job, 3 at a Task (JL 261030: "we don't have the studio/sNN, we just have sNN, along side with jNN, to make it visible directly"; "we might have many sNN, but we need to control the numbers").
Moved out on 261029: D08 is now b35-s07's s07-D01 (how topics connect); D09 is now b35-s05's s05-D01, rewritten as one drawing per leaf, holding its slides.
