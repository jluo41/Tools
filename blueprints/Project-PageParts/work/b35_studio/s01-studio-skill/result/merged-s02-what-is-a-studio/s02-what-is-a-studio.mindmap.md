# What is a Studio?

The total workflow first, then six open ideas, each with its slides. Each branch: the question, its options, the leaning, what is still open.

- [workflow] The total workflow <!-- n15 -->
  → Who says agreed; is a closed topic kept or deleted?
  - [agreed] A chat on any rung (Block, Job, Task) makes the topic, hung on that rung. <!-- n29 -->
  - [agreed] It holds Items, MindMap, Slides and passes (the previous chats). <!-- n30 -->
  - [agreed] Each round the Agent and the Human go round; Decided and result/ grow. <!-- n31 -->
  - [agreed] explore → shaping → agreed → closed; a round may loop back. <!-- n17 -->
  - [agreed] Only the agreed version goes, clean, to delivery/; then the topic closes. <!-- n32 -->
  - [open] Only JL says agreed? Now b35-s03. <!-- n16 -->
- [define] What is a Studio: a folder, a room, or the talk? <!-- n01 -->
  → Is "a Studio" one topic or all of a Block's topics?
  - [open] Today the word means both: `studio/` and one `sNN-<topic>/`. <!-- n02 -->
  - [option] A folder of drawings. <!-- n03 -->
  - [option] A room to think in, beside Report and Work. <!-- n04 -->
  - [leaning] The discussion itself: rounds on one open question. <!-- n05 -->
- [holds] One topic, five ways to look at it <!-- n06 -->
  → Is the face's Decided list bookkeeping?
  - [open] Decided may belong in Report. Now b35-s04. <!-- n07 -->
  - [leaning] Ideas first, as slides. <!-- n08 -->
  - [option] Drawing and Mind map: the same thinking, free and as a tree. <!-- n09 -->
  - [option] Rounds and the face stay small. <!-- n10 -->
- [inside] What is inside studio/? <!-- n23 -->
  → Are mock/ and assets/ allowed in a topic?
  - [open] b17-s04 keeps mock/, assets/ and five extra drawings the contract does not name. <!-- n24 -->
  - [agreed] studio/ holds one folder per topic, plus _build/ and loose drawings. <!-- n25 -->
  - [agreed] Source: face, ideas, slides.json, slides/, drawing, mind map, passes/. <!-- n26 -->
  - [agreed] With a builder: build_sNN_<topic>.py, its seed file, history/. <!-- n27 -->
  - [agreed] Made by scripts: .png, .mindmap.html, .slides.html, .slides.pptx. <!-- n28 -->
- [folders] One Block, four doors <!-- n11 -->
  → Does a Job or Task keep its own studio/?
  - [open] haipipe-delivery says every level (261028); its rule 1 says Block only. <!-- n12 -->
  - [agreed] related/ came in, studio/ think, reports/ answer, delivery/ went out. <!-- n13 -->
- [beside] The Studio thinks between in and out? <!-- n18 -->
  → Is a deck shown at a meeting a delivery?
  - [open] A meeting deck may be handed over. Now b35-s06. <!-- n19 -->
  - [option] A straight line: related/, studio/, delivery/. <!-- n20 -->
  - [leaning] Through Work and Report: the Studio never hands over itself. <!-- n21 -->
  - [option] The deck itself is a delivery. <!-- n22 -->
