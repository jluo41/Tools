## How do we define a Studio?
- A folder of drawings: the `studio/` folder a Block has today
- A room to think in, beside Report and Work
- The discussion itself: rounds of talk on one open question, until it settles

## What is inside a studio/ folder?
- `studio/`: one folder per topic, `sNN-<topic>/`; also `_build/` (shared builder helpers, make.sh) and loose drawings
- Source in a topic: face `sNN-<topic>.md`, `.ideas.md`, `.slides.json`, `slides/idea-NN-<slug>.png`, `.excalidraw`, `.mindmap.md`, `passes/pNN-MMDD/pass.md`
- When a script draws it: `build_sNN_<topic>.py`, `.sNN-<topic>.seed.json`, and `history/` for old builders
- Made by scripts: `.png`, `.mindmap.html`, `.slides.html`, `.slides.pptx` (the last three git-ignored)
leaning: the topic contract's list, nothing else; every source file starts with the topic's name

## What sits beside studio/ in a Block?
- `related/`: what came in (meetings, papers, files, emails)
- `studio/`: where we think
- `reports/`: the answers, one `qNN_<topic>/` each
- `delivery/`: what went out
leaning: one sNN numbering per Block
ask: Does a Job or Task keep its own studio/? haipipe-delivery says every level (261028), its rule 1 says Block only.

## What is a Studio's life?
flow: explore → shaping → agreed → closed
- explore: options appear, nothing chosen
- shaping: a leaning, still arguing
- agreed: a person says yes; it leaves for Work or Report
- closed: folded away, its answer lives in Report
ask: Who says agreed, only JL? Is a closed topic kept folded or deleted? (now asked in b35-s03)

## How does a Studio sit beside related/ and delivery/?
- A line: related/ feeds the Studio, the Studio feeds delivery/
- Through Work and Report: the Studio never hands over itself; an agreed idea goes to Work or Report, and those deliver
- The deck is a delivery: a Studio deck shown at a meeting is handed over
leaning: the second; related/ is what came in, delivery/ what went out, the Studio is where we think between them
