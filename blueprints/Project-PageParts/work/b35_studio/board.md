## Topic

**Studio: from idea to delivery** (JL 261030): one sNN, one idea, one delivery; an sNN ends in a deck (a must)
and the thing the deck is about. The Studio is where a person and the Agent go round on one thing until it is good: an open question,
a figure, a deck (JL 261029: "studio 它自己应该是一个新的 block"). It is a page part, not a Theme (JL 261030: "studio should not be the theme, it should be the
PageParts"), beside b31 excalidraw, b32 draft, b33 evidence and b34 display: every Theme uses it the same way: each sNN sits beside the Jobs or Tasks of the level it serves, no `studio/`
folder, at most 9 at a Block, 5 at a Job, 3 at a Task (b35-s01, JL 261030), a card in its level's Studio that every link to it opens (no page of its own, b35-s02-D05), slides, a mind map whose
every leaf has one drawing, rounds in `passes/`, working output in `result/`, and only the agreed
version handed to `delivery/`.

This Block holds the Studio, the mind map and the delivery together (JL 261030: "maybe we can put the studio,
mindmap, and delivery together as a block, right?"), since every sNN has one mind map and ends in one `delivery/`:
what a topic is and holds, how it lives from the first chat to closed, how it shows at each level, what its mind
map, slides and drawings carry, and what kinds of delivery exist. It excludes the work Theme's own page design (b17)
and each Theme's content; its answers change `haipipe-studio`, `haipipe-mindmap`, `haipipe-delivery`,
`haipipe-display` (`_parts/slide`) and the workbench's topic page.

## Deliveries

One sNN, one idea, one delivery of one type (b35-s01; JL 261030: "so each studio with a clear delivery, right? either
skills or draft, or display"; "is the delivery is not clean or is not one type, we will not treat it as a studio"):

- s01 · skill: a deck, and the haipipe-studio skill (SKILL.md's opening, `new_topic.py`'s `For:` line)
- s02 · ui: a deck, and the Studio's UI in the workbench: the Studio inside every page, and where a link to an sNN lands, its card (`focus_page.py`)
- s03 · skill: a deck, and the haipipe-mindmap skill: structure, node identity, drawings, slides and safe updates; retains one drawing per leaf
- s04 · skill: a deck, and a browsable Delivery Gallery page, example configuration and previews; the existing skill reference remains available
- s05 · skill: a deck, and a browsable Studio Gallery page, example configuration and links to the source Studios
- s08 · ui: a deck, and an interactive mind map page: edit a node, open its drawing, export the map
- s09 · skill: a deck, and the delivery skill's guide: where each delivery goes, how its version is kept, a handover checklist
- s21 · skill: a deck, and the map of b35's skills (haipipe-studio, haipipe-mindmap, haipipe-delivery, excalidraw-studio): each one's version, scripts and the sNN that change it
- s11 to s19 · ui: a deck each, and the rules for how the Studio part shows on a Block, a Job and a Task page, in its Report, Work and Studio (j02's t02 to t04, one cell per level and section, its Design the real page)
