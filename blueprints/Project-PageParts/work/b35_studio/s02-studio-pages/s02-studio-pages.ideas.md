## What is a topic for on each rung?
- Block: a design question for the whole Block
- Job: how its Tasks fit together, the plan
- Task: one figure, table or result to agree with a person
leaning: the three above (proposal 261029)
ask: Does a Job page also list its Tasks' topics?

## What does a reader see first on a topic page?
- Ideas: the slide, large, with its question
- Mind map: the whole topic as a tree
- Open: what the person owes
leaning: Ideas first, as the page does today
