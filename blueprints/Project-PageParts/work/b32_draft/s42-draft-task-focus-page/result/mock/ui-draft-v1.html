What changed, rule by rule

  1. Studio and Delivery leave the view-button-list for the right-rail. They are sections (think, hand over), not views of Work. Studio reads the topics that name this Task plus the paper's own; Delivery reads the Board's delivery/<job>/<task>/.Rule 2, one place one job; JL: "the studio should be in the right rail right?"
  2. Work's view-button-list is the five steps and Runs. Each carries its state as a word: sign, done, next, none called.Rule 5, every mark has a word; the old chips become words.
  3. goal-line (Aim) under the view-button-list, read from the face's reader-question:, the same in every view.Rule 1; the view-button-list stays right under the title-&-section (JL 261029).
  4. What waits for you is one row with its verb. Sign writes only the approved: line; until now you typed ✅ by hand. 🙋 New action, your call.Five-second test, question 4: what can I do.
  5. The prompt becomes a verb. Your notes in one line, then "Revise the plan" copies the prompt with them. The long prompt text stays off the page.No hints; JL 260927 "as concise as possible".
  6. The plan is drawn natively: paragraph, points, and its Scratch note right under it. No iframe, no fold, no card in a card.Rule 4, content first; JL: "so this is like something embeded here?"
  7. The old folded cards become one line each with Open, which opens a popup over the page.Rule 4: "list it or remove it"; a large view opens in a popup.
  8. Runs sit at the foot of their step; Work › Runs lists all of them. The right side now belongs to the right-rail.Replaces the squeezed vertical "Disk · Runs" tab.
  9. Every step has the same shape: waiting row, verbs, the result, its Runs.One pattern to learn, seven times.
  10. Revise keeps its text-left, cards-right reading, now with no box around the text and four switches above.JL 261021 rulings on Revise kept as they are.
  11. Studio's groups: This Section, Paper. One row per topic, picture left, at most three verbs.JL 261029: one item per row, no "All".
  12. Delivery: only you mark it sent. Check, then Mark sent writes the row in the Board's delivery.md.haipipe-delivery: only a person marks a delivery sent.
  13. Each built file is a row with its state word and Open (the preview in the popup, never inline).The four old boxes (build, render, send, read back) become verbs.
  14. right-rail lines say each section's verb (check, do, think, hand over); on a phone the right-rail shows as one line above the view-button-list.Rule 3; haipipe-ui Page parts 4.
