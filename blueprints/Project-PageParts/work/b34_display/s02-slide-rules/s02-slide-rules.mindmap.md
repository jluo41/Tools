# What makes a Studio slide?

How a deck is made first, then the rules a slide follows, then whether a meeting deck is a delivery.

- [workflow] The total workflow <!-- n01 -->
  → What makes a slide readable?
  - [agreed] Ideas in .ideas.md become slides in .slides.json. <!-- n02 -->
  - [agreed] Every slide is drawn in the deck's one style. <!-- n03 -->
  - [agreed] Each slide names its mind-map leaf. <!-- n04 -->
  - [agreed] topic_slides.py builds the .slides.html and .slides.pptx. <!-- n05 -->
- [rules] What makes a slide readable? <!-- n06 -->
  → Is a meeting deck a delivery?
  - [agreed] No contrast headline: ask plainly. <!-- n07 -->
  - [agreed] A whole life is one slide. <!-- n08 -->
  - [agreed] A flow names its actors and labels every arrow. <!-- n09 -->
  - [open] One style per Project, or per deck? <!-- n10 -->
- [meeting] Is a deck shown at a meeting a delivery? <!-- n11 -->
  - [open] Does a deck sent by email count differently? <!-- n12 -->
  - [option] Yes: it was handed to people. <!-- n13 -->
  - [leaning] No: a meeting is a round, a pass. <!-- n14 -->
  - [option] Only the agreed version, copied out after. <!-- n15 -->
