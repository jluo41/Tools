Every goal of this Block keeps these; a person signs them (run-setup-rules), and a new version is r<k+1>.
Each rule is checkable on the text alone. Placeholder rules for the prototype.

## Rules

- r1.1 at most 160 characters, the {NAME}, {DAY}, {TIME} and {LINK} slots not counted
- r1.2 opens with who it is from: {SENDER}
- r1.3 says how to stop: Reply STOP to opt out
- r1.4 plain words, no abbreviation the reader must decode

## Leave out

- any health, account or personal detail beyond the slots above
- urgency words (now, last chance, final notice)
