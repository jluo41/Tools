## Topic

The paper climbs three levels, a Board, a version (a Job) and a Section (a Task, a draft), and every page has three
sections with three aims: Report checks, Work does, Studio thinks (JL 261031: "we have the board, then job of the
paper version, and task of the section draft, I think it should be 3 x 3"). So the design is a 3 × 3 grid: one Task
per level, and in each Task one sNN per section. A Section's page is the draft Task page (b32-s42:draft @
draft_task_focus_page), live on the focus page; its sNN here say only what the paper shows on top.

```text
                 Report · check    Work · do    Studio · think
t01 Board        s32               s33          s34
t02 version      s35               s36          s37
t03 Section      s38               s39          s40
```
