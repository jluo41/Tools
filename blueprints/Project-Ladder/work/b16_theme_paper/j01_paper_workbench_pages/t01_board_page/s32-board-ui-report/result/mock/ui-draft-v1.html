What this section is, rule by rule

  1. Report checks. It shows what the paper says and how far it has got; it never asks you to act, except Open and Go.haipipe-ui: Report · check
  2. Board: Findings first. One row per question: its stage word, its answer's first sentence, Open to its report.A Board opens on Report (haipipe-ui).
  3. Version: Status lists Sections. Each Section's waiting step as a word; Go opens that step in the Section's Work.Which Section waits on me, at a glance.
  4. Section: Text at sentence level. Sentences left; their evidence, changes and open points right, read only.JL 261031: "sentence level, and the tracking, and the evidence"
  5. MindMap is content. Board: questions and versions; version: Sections and their paragraphs; Section: paragraphs, points, sentences.JL 261031: "the mindmap here should be the paper content"
  6. No Review view. The review report judges a pass, so it stays in Work › 5 · Revise.JL 261031: "the revise should be in the Work Section"
