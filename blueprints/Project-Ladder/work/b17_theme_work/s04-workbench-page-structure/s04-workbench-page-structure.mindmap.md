# What does every work page share?

The same at Block, Job and Task, around each level's Report, Work and Studio: the frame and the right-rail's other sections, six parts, one screen draft each in delivery/ui/, made by j03's t04_shared_parts.

- [frame] The frame every work page wears <!-- n01 -->
  → Does it read the same at every level?
  - [agreed] path: the SPACE down to this page. <!-- n02 -->
  - [agreed] title-&-section: its name, then the open section. <!-- n03 -->
  - [agreed] view-button-list: the open section's views, under the title. <!-- n04 -->
  - [agreed] goal-line: Purpose, Goal or Aim, by level. <!-- n05 -->
  - [agreed] view-caption: what the open view shows. <!-- n06 -->
  - [agreed] left-rail: change page; siblings, then the open page's children. <!-- n07 -->
  - [agreed] right-rail: change section; one line each. <!-- n08 -->
  - [agreed] pop-out: a Run, a file or a topic's part, over the page. <!-- n09 -->
  - [agreed] phone: the rails hide; one section-line on top. <!-- n10 -->
- [rail] The right-rail's other sections <!-- n11 -->
  → What does each show, at every level?
  - [agreed] Interactions, answer: every form at this page or below. <!-- n12 -->
  - [agreed] Delivery, hand over: what this page hands over. <!-- n13 -->
  - [agreed] Related, take in: meetings, papers, emails. <!-- n14 -->
  - [agreed] Timeline, look back: what happened when. <!-- n15 -->
  - [agreed] Files, browse: the folder itself. <!-- n16 -->
- [open] What is still open <!-- n17 -->
  → Which way do we lean?
  - [open] Which Interactions page: A, B or C? <!-- n18 -->
    A a questionnaire grouped by the page that asked; B two lanes picked by their pictures; C a checklist with one form open at a time (my pick). The form runs/r02_collect_interactions_page waits.
  - [open] An empty section: stay on the rail, or leave? <!-- n19 -->
    Interactions and Delivery stay today; Studio, Related and Timeline leave. The Job and Task Studio forms ask it (my pick: stay).
  - [open] The rail's Delivery beside the Report's type views? <!-- n20 -->
    Leaning: the rail keeps the files, versions and sent marks; the Report keeps the look and one word.

  - [Job] s54 Report · s55 Work · s56 Studio. <!-- n23 -->
  - [Task] s57 Report · s58 Work · s59 Studio. <!-- n24 -->
