# s06 · The design workflow: ideas

One idea per slide, in the deck's order; each slide is its own Excalidraw drawing in `slides/` (build_slides.py).
Full names on every slide, never short ids.

## What does a design Job make?
- one goal × one method version × one inputs version → a set of designs; the Job pins all three
## The goal comes first
- written once at the Block, signed by you, pinned by each Job, in its fence, read by every check and the ranking
## One change at a time
- a new goal, inputs version or method version is a new Job; a released Job is never edited
## A · Set up the Block, once
- you sign the goal and the shared rules; an inputs version is frozen; a method version comes from the skill
## B · Launch a Job behind a fence
- pin all three, reason ideas only from the fence, another agent checks it
## Every idea gets its Task
- five more ideas than the goal asks for; opening never chooses
## C · Draft, check, redraft
- the designer drafts, another agent checks rules, sources and a critique; a fail is a new draft
## A design's state walk
- draft → checking → redraft → passed → kept → released, or dropped
## Out of the fence is not a redraft
- drop it, or a new inputs version and a new Job
## D · Rank, keep the best, predict
- percentage points against the control, with a range and its why
## Review and approve, then close
- one sign freezes the predictions and releases; close once every design is released or dropped
## The Map: where Jobs are compared
- goals × methods; two Jobs in one cell compare; a held Job says why
## E · Learn: the experiment starts the next round
- totals per arm at the Block, scored by the reviewer agent; new inputs; the scorecard; a method change you sign
## Who does what
- eighteen steps: the designer agent makes nine, the reviewer agent checks four, you sign five
