s05 · The insight workflow: from a plan and a data freeze to a signed handoff
=============================================================================

**Tags:** `Workflow`

**Stage:** explore

**For:** a deck, and the insight workflow as one map (who does each step, what it writes, the screen it lives on), for the people who write the insight skills and build its workbench

**Delivery type:** skill

**Covers:** the whole insight workflow in five zones: A make the plan (01-04) · B run the Job (05-08) · C answer up the levels (09-10) · D compare and close (11-13) · E across Jobs, then hand off (14-16); the three loops back (a returned CHECK, proposals into the next plan, a new data freeze); what each step writes; how an answer holds.

**Excluded:** the screens themselves (j01's 3 × 3, s51 to s59); the ladder's folders (s01, s04); each Run's card and skill (s21; haipipe-insight-workflow ref/run-cards.md); the DIKW methods (s03).

**Topic:** one map of how a question travels, as b15 s03 draws labeling's complete workflow (JL 261101: "we should have a studio to understand what is the overall workflow"). Insight answers what one dataset can and cannot say on two clocks: the plan moves in the Prototype, the data moves on the Board, and each Job pins one of each. Agents plan, run, write and check; a person sets the cuts and signs the plan, closes each Job and signs the handoff. Each card names its Runs and the 3 × 3 screen it lives on, so the map also reads the UI: a step with no screen shows. The steps are haipipe-insight ref/insight-ladder.md's rules and Runs, in the order a question travels; the words are written once in `s05-insight-workflow-workflow.yaml`.

**Feeds:** `reports/` q01_insight_ladder · q03_question_gates · q04_level_delivery
