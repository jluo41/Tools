Block folders

The Work Folder: the work itself. Each Block holds three kinds of numbered folders side by side: jNN Jobs, sNN Studio topics and cNN Chats. Insight has three Blocks: the Question Book, the DataPrep Block and the Board.

  • jNN_<job>/Jobthe work: Job, Task, Run, Result
  • sNN-<topic>/Studioa topic thought through and drawn
  • cNN_<slug>/Chatone kept session, numbered in time order
