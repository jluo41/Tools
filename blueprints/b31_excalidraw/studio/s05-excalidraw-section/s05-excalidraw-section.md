**Topic:** what the skill `excalidraw-draft` draws and how: a paper's Sections (or one Section) as a map read
left to right, the paper, its groups, its Sections in board order, each Section's paragraphs, each Bullet a row,
then a Text column left empty and an Evidence column with a card per Evidence Item. The map is generated by
`ref/excalidraw_section.py` from the Draft plans and Evidence Items, never edited by hand. This topic draws the
skill's parts, its workflow, its folder, and the one real map it has made, so a session knows what the map
holds and what it leaves out before it redraws one.

What the drawing holds
----------------------

Four frames in two acts, a row each, the act named at the left, a named arrow for each step (b31 s01 principle ③).
Facts (version, functions, counts, the sample map's boxes) are read from disk on every build; `{name}` in a
yaml string below is filled by the builder.

```yaml
acts:
- name: Act 1 · What it draws
  frames: [①, ②]
  arrow: drawn by
- name: Act 2 · Where it lives and what it made
  frames: [③, ④]
  arrow: made
between: {from: ①, to: ③, label: kept in}
```

```text
Act 1 · What it draws          ① the map, one mind map ─drawn by─▶ ② the workflow, plans to map
         │ kept in
Act 2 · Where it lives         ③ its folder, as a tree ─made─▶ ④ the one real map, checked
```

① 🧠 the skill as a mind map: centre "what it draws"; six branches: the columns, where its words come from, the
   colours, the depths, what it never does, its script.
② 🔁 the workflow: the inputs (board, plans, evidence items) into the script, then what a run does step by
   step, and the guard that refuses to replace a hand-edited map.
③ 🧰 the skill folder and the folder its maps are written into, both read from disk, and who owns each input.
④ 🖼️ the one real paper map, its preview, checked against the drawing principles, one line each; red where it fails.

Rebuild: `python build_s05_excalidraw_section.py`, then `render_png.py` for the preview.

The workflow (②)
----------------

```yaml
flow_in:
  labels: [read by, read by, called by, writes, listed by]
  steps:
  - title: 📋 board.md
    lines: ["## Pages gives the Section order and the groups", "paper_groups() reads it; Story is dropped"]
  - title: 📝 Draft plan
    lines: ["draft/<stem>-draft-v<G>.<S>.md", "## 1 Structure: paragraphs, Bullets, roles", "latest_outline() picks the newest"]
  - title: 🧾 Evidence Items
    lines: ["draft/<stem>-evidence-items.md", "one row per item: target, need, verified", "read_items() groups them by Bullet"]
  - title: 🐍 excalidraw_section.py
    key: true
    lines: ["section_node() then layout()", "one column a level, a row a Bullet", "cards from draw-logic-tree"]
  - title: 🗺️ the map
    lines: ["<paper>/studio/", "paper-sections.excalidraw", "source names the script"]
flow_run:
  labels: [then, then, then, then]
  steps:
  - title: ⌨️ 1 · Parse
    lines: ["a page.md, or --paper <folder>", "--depth section|paragraph|bullet|evidence"]
  - title: 🌳 2 · Build the tree
    lines: ["node(): paper, group, Section, part, paragraph, Bullet", "no plan: a dashed grey Section"]
  - title: 📐 3 · Lay out
    lines: ["layout(): x by kind, y by row", "a Bullet's row grows to hold its cards"]
  - title: 🛑 4 · Guard
    lines: ["edited(): any element past version 1", "refused unless --force (--check skips it)"]
  - title: 💾 5 · Write
    lines: ["json to studio/", "prints boxes, cards, elements"]
```
