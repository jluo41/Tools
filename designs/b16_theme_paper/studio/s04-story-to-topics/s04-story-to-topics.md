Decided
-------

s04-D01 · (JL 261007) The Story and Ideation Pages stop being Pages. A paper Board is b03's shape:
    a face (board.md) with its Questions, studio/ topics, reports/ answers, runs/.
s04-D02 · (JL 261007) Ideation is a studio topic, `studio/s01-ideation/`; each telling of the paper
    (one per Story today) is a studio topic `studio/sNN-story-<telling>/`: its identity, pitch and
    stakes in the face, its drawings beside it. An earlier telling is a topic that has ended.
s04-D03 · (JL 261007) Each research question is a Board Question in `board.md ## Questions`, its
    `group:` the topic it belongs to, answered by `reports/qNN_<question>/` (Answer · Evidence ·
    Limits · Next). A Story's evidence, discovery and Task roadmaps become each report's Work.
s04-D04 · (JL 261007) The Story and Ideation skills stay; they are called from the Idea Studio and
    Audience Report as run types, and they write a topic or register a Question.
s04-D05 · The Section Narrative and the compile order go to the version that tells the story: its
    face's `## Narrative`. The build reads its order there.


Open
----

1. A Board that still has Story Pages: every reader reads the new homes first and the Story Page
   until every paper Board has moved.
2. An earlier telling's questions, sent and judged: their own group, answered from that send?
3. Review comments: their points become Questions in a reviewer group, answered by the next version.
4. Each version keeps its own Narrative and order, or only the newest? (Built: each version's face keeps
   its own; a new version starts with a copy of the one it follows.)

Settled since (JL 261007): a review batch is a report of type comments, in the version that answers it
(`reports/qNN_<kind>-<MMDD>/`, `page-type: comments`), so open point 3 has its home.
