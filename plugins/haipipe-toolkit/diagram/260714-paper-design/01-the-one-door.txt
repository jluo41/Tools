1 — THE ONE DOOR
=================

  Files here:  01 the one door   02 the four phases
               03 before / after   04 the edit set

  Approved JL 2026-07-14. Edits pending.
  Builds directly on 260714-probe-qa (read that first — this is one ruling on top of it).


The ruling, in one sentence.
----------------------------

    A paper — and an application — may call ONLY the probe.

Not the task layer.
Not the discovery layer.
The probe, and nothing else.


What that removes
-----------------

The paper front door has two escape hatches today:

    /haipipe-paper discover "..."   ──▶  /haipipe-discovery
    /haipipe-paper task "..."       ──▶  /haipipe-task

The application front door has the same two. Both are labelled
"non-claim utility work (lit scan, data check)."

Both are DELETED.

The paper skill will no longer proxy a call into the bank.
There is no "non-claim" exception. There is no small enough need.


Why — this is the same wound as C6/C7
--------------------------------------

260714-probe-qa, LAW 2:

    "The C6/C7 leak happened with NO probe file involved anywhere.
     A paper session, holding the stake in its context, simply went
     and did the task work itself."

The `discover` / `task` verbs are the PAVED ROAD to that leak.

```text
   ❌  /haipipe-paper task "quick recon on the WellDoc cycle field"
           │
           ▼
       a paper session, stake in context, drives /haipipe-task itself
       → the QA file comes back shaped around THIS paper's hypotheses
       → the bank is contaminated for the next paper
```

The wall (LAW 2) says a consumer NEVER executes bank work inline.
But the front door was handing out a verb that does exactly that.

We were policing the act while advertising the shortcut.
Close the shortcut.


JL's two lines, decoded
-----------------------

  > "maybe we don't call the discover and task from the paper side.
  >  It is the probe's task."

    Calling the bank is the PROBE's job, not the paper's.
    The paper raises a question; the probe decides reuse-vs-dispatch
    and — only through a clean-context agent — reaches the bank.

  > "what we want are more like the stages' four phases."

    What the front door SHOULD expose is stages, and inside each stage
    the four phases DRAFT · PROBE · REVISE · CHECK.
    Evidence enters through the PROBE phase. There is no separate
    top-level verb that jumps the bank. See file 02.


What does NOT change
--------------------

The bank still has its own front door, for humans and orchestrators:

    /haipipe-task qa "what about the female subset?"     ──▶  QA/3-female-subset.md
    /haipipe-discovery qa "CGM landscape?"               ──▶  QA/...

260714-probe-qa already blesses this ("Two people can explore a task.
Neither of them is a probe."). That door stays open.

The difference is WHO knocks:

    ✅  a human types  /haipipe-task qa "..."     — a person using the bank
    ❌  a paper types  /haipipe-paper task "..."  — the paper proxying the bank

The first is a human at the bank's counter.
The second is the paper reaching over the counter.
We are removing the second, only.
