⛔ GATE 1 — THE DRAFT GATE
==========================

  The most valuable stop in the lifecycle, and the one the paper lacks today.
  The human approves FOUR things:

       (a) 🎯 the DEMAND         — is this really what the claims will need?
       (b) ✂️  the SCOPE CUTS     — Q4's MLM arm doesn't exist. Cut H1's MLM clause?
       (c) 🌍 CROSS-PROJECT REUSE — ✅ RULED: authorized HERE (C4, 05-rulings.txt)
       (d) 💰 the COST of every BUILD about to be commissioned

  ‼️  NOTHING EXPENSIVE IS DISPATCHED BEFORE THAT LINE EXISTS IN _LOG.

  💡 a scope cut HERE is FREE. After claims it costs a CLAIM.
     After display it costs a FIGURE.

⛔ GATE 2 — THE CHECK GATE
==========================

  Does a 3-week build block the stage?  ❌ NO.

     ┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
     │ "Does every hypothesis have a resource that is HAVE+FIT,               │
     │  or a COMMISSIONED build with an owner and a DATE,                     │
     │  or a SCOPE CUT the human said out loud?"                              │
     └────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

     ❌ NOT "are the resources BUILT?" — unanswerable when a build takes 3 weeks.
     ✅ Answerable in MINUTES even when the work takes WEEKS.

  ‼️  This is the load-bearing sentence of the whole stage. If the gate had asked
     "are they built?", the stage would block every paper behind its slowest
     dependency — and would have been abandoned in a month.


🛣️  TWO LANES
=============

```text
   🔎 SCAN    minutes      🔴 GATE-BLOCKING     ⬅ what makes the stage DECIDABLE
              ‼️  a SCAN question whose route exceeds ~1 HOUR is MISFILED.
                 It is a BUILD wearing a SCAN's clothes. Re-file it.

   🏗️  BUILD   days–weeks   🟢 NON-BLOCKING      state: commissioned
              required:  owner · eta (YYYY-MM-DD) · blocks · cross-project
              ✅ eta in the FUTURE, no answer yet   → PASSES the gate
              ❌ eta PASSED, still no answer        → HARD FAIL (C6)
```

  ⚠️  WHY THE DATE IS MANDATORY (C6): without a date check, `commissioned` becomes
     the status EVERY un-run question wears, and the whole feature ships as a
     LAUNDERING MECHANISM. The eta is the price of the non-blocking lane.
     `date -d "$eta"` is a machine test, enforced by check-probe-cards.sh.
