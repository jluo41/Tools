## 0.1.2 - 2026-08-10

Formal Paper Display now has an explicit upstream: same-numbered candidate cards paired with
every Value or Literature probe. Narrative selects a candidate against a named claim; only that
selection may open a requested formal unit. Candidate exploration and human acceptance of the
shipped float remain separate.

## 0.1.1 - 2026-08-05

Review fixes, no rule change:

- The opener names this type's own risk, acceptance of a specific render,
  instead of sharing a byte-identical first line with `-for-design`.
- The REQUIRED `page-type: display` frontmatter key is stated: display units
  wear stage filenames, and the key beats the filename (base type resolution ③).
- Plain English: the placement rule now reads "A unit that renders well but is
  cited by no sentence is not finished."

## 0.1.0 - 2026-08-05

**Created on JL's ruling** (QB6 Decision Now, option D + display standing alone:
"how about the -for-literature, and -for-values, and -for-display, I want to
include them as well").

The topic types load ONE shared core, `haipipe-board/ref/topic-entry-contract.md`,
and add only their route's translation layer, so the register and entry anatomy

BY the project and closes on human acceptance, not on shipping.
