By reader expectations
======================

One writing method card. The Paper workbench shows it in the shared Guide › Method,
under Writing methods; its papers are the rows of `../../paper-papers.md` whose
`group` is `by reader expectations`. A claim names its source in brackets; "(ours)" marks the
workbench's own judgment.

family: Writing and response: How is it told, and defended?
move: Order each Section, paragraph and sentence for its reader: the known first, the
  new and important last; the Section's order follows the Story's.
comes from: Gopen & Swan 1990, the science of scientific writing
reads: the Story's order · each Section's plan
returns: Section Pages in the order the reader needs them
test now: T4 independent
test in use: T5 answered


What the literature says
------------------------

rationale: Readers take meaning from where information sits; putting it where they
  expect it makes complex science clear without simplifying it [Gopen 1990].
context: Scientific writing [Gopen 1990].
steps: 1. Order the Sections by the Story. 2. Give each Section its part of the
  argument. 3. Write each Page by the Page method.
strengths: The same rule works at the scale of the paper, the Section and the sentence
  (ours).
limitations: It shapes how the argument reads, not whether it holds (ours).


Applied to AI
-------------

agent: Each Section is a Page written through the Page Workbench.
steps: 1. Plan the Section from the Story. 2. Write and check it as a Page.
returns: one released Section Page per Section.
verify: Each Section's Page check passed (T4).
risk: Sections written separately can repeat or contradict each other; the Narrative
  review reads them together (ours).
evidence on ai: No study tests these rules on agent-written Sections (ours).
