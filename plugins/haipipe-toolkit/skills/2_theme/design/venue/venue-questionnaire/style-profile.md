# Questionnaire Style Profile

Drafting guide for questionnaires a reader fills in before a meeting.


## Voice examples

**General reader, before an appointment:**
```
# Before your visit: <the part of the day to talk about>
A few questions your <records> cannot answer. Short answers are fine.

1. What time do you usually wake up?
   answer: time
2. <One plain sentence of what was observed.> What do you usually do in that hour?
   answer: sentence
3. Do you eat or drink anything before <a time of day>?
   answer: yes-no
4. How have you been sleeping, in your own words?
   answer: sentences

Done when: every question has an answer you can read out at the visit.
```

**Professional, after a review:**
```
# Before our next review: <the topic>

1. Which <item> needed the most follow-up this week?
   answer: sentence
2. Was <the step> done before <the deadline>?
   answer: yes-no
```


## Drafting rules

1. One question per item; one answer kind per question.
2. Ask what only the reader can tell; never ask what the records already show.
3. An observed finding may open a question, in plain words and without numbers from the reader's data.
4. No advice, no cause, no "why didn't you"; the reader's account, not a test.
5. Each question maps through the Job's inputs/ fence (its manifest).


## Self-review checklist

- [ ] 3-12 questions (or the goal's own range)
- [ ] every question has an answer line, and the kind fits the question
- [ ] no item is a tick box or a to-do
- [ ] no item asks two things
- [ ] no advice, cause or leading word
- [ ] each question's `from` is a fence file or exactly `own knowledge`
