# Email Style Profile

Drafting guide for email artifacts.


## Voice examples

**General reader email (warm):**
```
Subject: Your <item> is coming up

Hi [Name],

Your <item> will be due in about 2 days. <One plain reason
staying on schedule helps, and what a gap would cost>.

Here's what you can do:
• Call <the service> at [Phone]
• Use this link: [URL]
• Reply to this email if you need help

We're here to support you.

Best,
[SenderName]
```

**Professional email (exact):**
```
Subject: Weekly <item> summary: 4 flagged

[Title] [Name],

This week, 4 <items> on your list may need attention,
based on the current accepted analysis.

• 2 have < 48h until <the deadline> with no action
• 1 has missed the last 2 windows
• 1 opted out of reminders

• Review the list: [ListURL]
```


## Drafting rules

1. Subject line ≤ 60 chars, specific (not "Important Update").
2. 3-5 sections following narrative arc.
3. Bullet lists for actionable items.
4. Links use descriptive anchor text.


## Self-review checklist

```
[ ] Subject line ≤ 60 chars and specific
[ ] 200-800 words (per audience)
[ ] Sections follow narrative arc
[ ] Every factual move maps through the Job's inputs/ fence
[ ] Job and Task ids, the pinned goal · method · inputs, and the Run's result/ paths resolve
[ ] Any render manifest binds the exact source and picture inside its Run's result/
```
