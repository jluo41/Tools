# Journal of Imaginary Systems (JIS) Playbook

🚫 FABRICATED. The Journal of Imaginary Systems does not exist. No editor
published these rules, no paper was ever submitted here, and nothing in this
folder may be cited or followed. The pack exists so that `QBt2-for-venue.md` can
be a REAL venue page whose bindings all resolve while every fact stays invented.
A wrong word cap that reads as real could cost a real submission, which is why
the marking sits on every file in this pack and inside every figure that carries

A `playbook-jis` **style + structure blueprint pack** for the paper pipeline. It
holds what a JIS Observation is built out of, section by section, plus the desk
taste an editor screens on. It is a blueprint pack and NOT a measured one: the
exemplar corpus does not exist, so nothing here is a measurement of published
papers.

## How to use

At the claims, display, and section-edit stages, read this pack for the
JIS-shaped target for that artifact. Read `taste.md` first when the question is
whether this desk would look at the paper at all. Read `style-profile.md` when
the question is what a block owes. Read the DESK for anything that binds.

## Maps to lifecycle stages

### to Claims

- Exactly ONE `[primary]` claim, and at JIS it must be a MECHANISM claim that
  someone else could recount from the deposited artifact.
- The primary claim's verb is capped by the design, not by the writing. An
  observational design supports "is associated with" and never "reduces".
- 1 to 3 supporting claims, each with its own row in the corpus table.

### to Display

JIS standard display set:
- **One count table** carrying the bands, the denominators, and the interval.
  This is the HERO and it is tied to the `[primary]` claim.
- **One provenance line under every display**, naming the command that
  regenerates it. The desk enforces this and the pack only repeats it.
- Mapping rule: one claim, one display, one command.

### to Minimap

A JIS Observation follows a ceiling-forward arc, not IMRAD:

```
Question -> What this design cannot say -> Why it matters -> How it was counted -> What came out
```

The desk's reading order puts the Limits block SECOND, directly after the
abstract, which is the one structural thing that makes a JIS paper look
different from every sibling. Writing Limits last and reading it second is the
normal way to produce it.

### to Write / Edit

Imitate the shapes in `style-profile.md`. There is no exemplar corpus to imitate
sentences from, so the pack gives slot patterns and refusals only.

## The family delta

Three desks share this blueprint, and the outlet is chosen on the rows where
they differ.

```
  desk   primary claim                       reading order        deposit
  ────   ─────────────────────────────────   ──────────────────   ──────────────
  JIS    a mechanism someone can recount     Limits SECOND        required
  TIS    a structure, proved or characterized   Limits LAST       not asked for
  PIW    an early observation, no mechanism  no Limits block      optional

  ⚖️ TIE-BREAK ORDER   is there a mechanism · is there a recountable artifact
     mechanism + artifact                              JIS
     mechanism, no artifact, formal instead            TIS
     neither, and the point is that the thing exists   PIW
```
