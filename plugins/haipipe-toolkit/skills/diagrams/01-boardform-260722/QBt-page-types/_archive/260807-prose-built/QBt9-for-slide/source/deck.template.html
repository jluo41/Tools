What the design lets the talk say

Association, not reduces

① tenure is not assigned
a page declared its key whenever its author got to it, so early declarers may differ from late ones in ways this census cannot see
② size is not controlled
longer pages carry more sections, and more sections are more places to drift

So the title's question is the one thing this figure cannot answer. The gradient is in the data; the causal claim is not available, and the talk says so before anyone asks.
