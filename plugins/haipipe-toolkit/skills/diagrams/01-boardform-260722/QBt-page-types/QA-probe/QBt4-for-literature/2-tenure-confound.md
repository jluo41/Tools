## Question

Has any published design ASSIGNED a page's type key, rather than observing pages
whose authors declared one whenever they got to it? What is the strongest
published design on this question, and what verb does it license a drift finding
to use?

The ceiling therefore holds from outside as well as inside. This paper's figure
