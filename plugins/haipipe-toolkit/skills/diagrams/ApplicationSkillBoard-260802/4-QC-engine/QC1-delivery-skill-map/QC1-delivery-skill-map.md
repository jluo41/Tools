state: ✅ SETTLED · Application 0.8.0 routes shipped

## Opening

Which public route owns each kind of Application work?

The router diagnoses a Page frontier, then hands authority to one Page Type or a delivery-tail procedure. Legacy stage verbs are compatibility reads and may not recreate the former ladder as a new Application.

### Writing Style

Map user language to the user-facing Page name. Put the machine key in parentheses only when needed.

## Content

### 1 · Contract

**Contract map**: the subparts below refine the Page decision into one bounded handoff.

```text
inputs → bounded contract → observable handoff
```

Creates or repairs the single Brief and its Insight Need Map/Design roster.

#### 2 · Insight route

Creates or refreshes one local Insight Page. It may use PageX and, under
Task-backed authority, Probe.

#### 3 · Design route

Creates or repairs one audience/job/venue Design Page. It consumes the Brief and accepted Design Handoffs through PageX and never Probes.

#### 4 · Artifact route

Runs the independent-governance admission test before creating a Page. Most requested outputs remain Design projections.

#### 5 · Missing knowledge

The router shows the blocked Aim, question, target, PageX matches, destination local Insight Page, and reason for release. It does not silently commission new evidence work.
