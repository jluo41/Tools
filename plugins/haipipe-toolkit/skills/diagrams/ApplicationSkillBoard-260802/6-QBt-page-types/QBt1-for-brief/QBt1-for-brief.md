## Opening
What must be settled before an intervention can be designed without borrowing Paper's Opening type?

Brief binds opportunity, audience, behavior, outcome, selected Insight Pages, venue, and promise in one Application-owned Page. Its distinct name keeps the global resolver unambiguous and gives Intervention one bounded handoff.

**Covered here**: the Application identity-and-promise Page Type.

**Covered elsewhere**: TaskSkillBoard QD3 settles DIKW; QBt2 owns the intervention architecture.

## Writing Style
Use audience and behavior language. Cite Insight Pages by PageX path and never restate their evidence chain.

## Diagram
**Brief's place**: settled knowledge becomes an application promise, not another analysis.

```text
Insight Pages ── PageX ──▶ Brief ── accepted handoff ──▶ Intervention
```

## Content

### 1 · Opportunity
**Opportunity boundary**: the problem is important, bounded, and evidence-linked.
```text
observed problem + affected audience + consequence → opportunity
```
The intervention addresses one bounded problem whose importance is supported by selected Insight Pages.

### 2 · Audience and Behavior
**Behavior frame**: one actor moves from a current action to a desired action in context.
```text
who | context | current behavior → desired behavior
```
The Page names who acts, in what context, the current behavior, and the desired change.

### 3 · Outcome and Kill Criteria
**Decision frame**: success and stopping are observable before design begins.
```text
success metric | guardrail | kill condition | horizon
```
Success, safety limits, and conditions for stopping are observable before design begins.

### 4 · Selected Insights
**Selection ledger**: every adopted finding keeps its strength and boundary.
```text
PageX path | K/W row | relevance | strength | boundary
```
Each selected PageX binding states relevance, strength, and boundary; absent evidence becomes a missing-Insight request.

### 5 · Venue
**Venue pin**: the Brief selects a pack without copying its rules.
```text
audience context + behavior moment → venue pack
```
The Page pins channel and format from an Application venue pack without duplicating the pack.

### 6 · Promise
**Promise ceiling**: audience-facing language cannot exceed selected knowledge.
```text
opportunity + selected K/W + venue → bounded promise
```
The promise is audience-facing and cannot exceed the selected Insight limits.

### 7 · Intervention Handoff
**Brief packet**: one accepted boundary opens composition.
```text
audience + behavior + outcome + venue + promise + constraints
```
One bounded packet carries audience, behavior, outcome, venue, promise, sources, and unresolved constraints.
