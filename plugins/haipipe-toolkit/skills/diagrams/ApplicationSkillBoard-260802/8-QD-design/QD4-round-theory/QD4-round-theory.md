## Opening

Why does the design round have these phases, and not a house convention someone liked?

Because each phase is the operational form of an established finding. The round is `haipipe-design-workflow`'s D0-D4, plus the D5 PageDown seal added 260828: frame a brief, bet a card, realize units in parallel, evaluate each with two verdicts, accept or emit a question, then page the round down: the grown pages reread as documents before the round may close. D5's literature slot is OPEN: it was added from a live failure (six rounds froze the pages at round 1), and its anchors, likely the documentation-decay and living-document literatures, are owed a discovery sweep of their own. A 260827 discovery sweep verified 59 sources behind those choices, every citation resolved live against Crossref, OpenAlex or arXiv, and this page registers the anchors and points at the five QA files that carry the full lists. The skill itself stays lean and cites this page, never the papers.

### Writing Style

Register, never restate: an anchor row here is authors, year, venue, id and one clause; the argument lives in the QA files. Only verified sources may appear, and an exclusion is recorded, not papered over.

## Content

### 1 · Frame: satisficing, not completeness

**Why GD0 asks "enough to frame", never "everything"**: the anchor rows.

```text
Simon 1956            Psychological Review    10.1037/h0042769
Simon 1973            Artificial Intelligence 10.1016/0004-3702(73)90011-8
Simon 1996 [1969]     MIT Press               10.7551/mitpress/12107.001.0001
Paton & Dorst 2011    Design Studies          10.1016/j.destud.2011.07.002
Restrepo & Christiaans 2004  J. Design Research  10.1504/jdr.2004.009842
```

Bounded-rationality agents act on a workable structure, not a covered topic; designers stop seeking information once one exists, experts sooner than novices; and the brief is elaborated alongside early concept work rather than finalized upfront. That is D0's gate wording verbatim: framed is enough. The full seventeen, including the information-sufficiency studies, sit in QA 01 (`01_brief-framing-and-satisficing`).

#### 2 · Bet: abduction on record

**Why a card declares stance, thesis and expected effect before any copy exists**:

```text
Dorst 2011            Design Studies          10.1016/j.destud.2011.07.006
Conklin & Begeman 1988  CSCW '88              10.1145/62266.62278
MacLean et al. 1991   Human-Computer Interaction  10.1207/s15327051hci0603&4_2
Nosek et al. 2018     PNAS                    10.1073/pnas.1708274114
Cockburn et al. 2018  CHI                     10.1145/3173574.3173715
Gaver 2012            CHI · the counterpoint  10.1145/2207676.2208538
```

A design direction is an abductive bet; IBIS and QOC captured positions with their arguments before commitment; preregistration fixes the expectation before the outcome is known, which is exactly what blocks a wager placed after seeing the result. Gaver's counterpoint, that not every artifact should be forced into a hypothesis frame, is kept on the record: the `ignore` stance and record mode are the family's answer to it. Full list in QA 02 (`02_rationale-capture-and-prereg`).

#### 3 · Realize: parallel beats serial

**Why one arm-agent per released card, fanned out**:

```text
Dow et al. 2010       ACM TOCHI               10.1145/1879831.1879836
Jansson & Smith 1991  Design Studies          10.1016/0142-694X(91)90003-F
Diehl & Stroebe 1987  J. Personality & Social Psych.  10.1037/0022-3514.53.3.497
Girotra et al. 2010   Management Science      10.1287/mnsc.1090.1144
Toh & Miller 2016     Research in Eng. Design 10.1007/s00163-015-0212-1
```

The parallel-vs-serial RCT found parallel wins on quality, divergence and self-efficacy; fixation shows why one early artifact narrows everything after it; production blocking shows why serial turn-taking generates less; and risk aversion at concept selection is an argument for keeping the accept decision a person's, separate from generation. Full list in QA 03 (`03_parallel-vs-serial-prototyping`).

#### 4 · Evaluate: two verdicts, two time directions

**Why D3 mandates reflect (ex-post) AND prospect (ex-ante)**:

```text
Venable et al. 2016   European J. of IS · FEDS  10.1057/ejis.2014.36
Nielsen & Molich 1990 CHI '90                 10.1145/97243.97281
Polson et al. 1992    Int. J. Man-Machine Studies  10.1016/0020-7373(92)90039-n
Carroll 2000          Interacting with Computers   10.1016/s0953-5438(00)00023-0
Park et al. 2023      UIST                    10.1145/3586183.3606763
Argyle et al. 2023    Political Analysis      10.1017/pan.2023.2
Hämäläinen et al. 2023  CHI · the caution     10.1145/3544548.3580688
```

FEDS names the axis this family's alias law adopts: ex-ante artificial evaluation before deployment, ex-post naturalistic after. Inspection methods ground reflect; scenario-based design grounds the walkthrough inside prospect; the generative-agent line grounds simulated-audience prospecting, with the CHI 2023 caution, synthetic respondents are plausible but systematically homogeneous, standing behind guardrail ②: a forecast is never a claim. Full list in QA 04 (`04_ex-ante-ex-post-evaluation`).

#### 5 · Turn: co-evolution and calibration

**Why round one always completes, why EMIT is the only insufficiency exit, and why forecasts are scored**:

```text
Rittel & Webber 1973  Policy Sciences         10.1007/BF01405730
Schön 1992            Knowledge-Based Systems 10.1016/0950-7051(92)90020-G
Dorst & Cross 2001    Design Studies          10.1016/s0142-694x(01)00009-6
Hevner et al. 2004    MIS Quarterly           10.2307/25148625
Hevner 2007           Scandinavian J. of IS   aisel.aisnet.org/sjis/vol19/iss2/4
Brier 1950            Monthly Weather Review  10.1175/1520-0493(1950)078<0001:VOFEIT>2.0.CO;2
Mellers et al. 2014   Psychological Science   10.1177/0956797614524255
```

Wicked problems are defined by their candidate solutions; the move talks back, so designing is itself the inquiry that reveals the missing insight; problem and solution co-evolve, which the EMIT edge makes mechanical; the DSR three-cycle view is the macro frame the two boards realize; and the forecasting-tournament line shows calibration is measurable and improvable, which is what the outer loop's Brier-style scoring buys the next round's bets. Full list in QA 05 (`05_iterative-co-evolution-and-calibration`).
