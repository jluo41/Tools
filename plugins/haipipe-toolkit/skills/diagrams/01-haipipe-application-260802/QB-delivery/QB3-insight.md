## Opening

What does rung 1b-themes deliver as the ladder's Insight step, and what grounds a theme?
A theme is a pattern worth testing, such as evening reminders going unread, and grounding is the described data entry or outside source it must cite.
Patterns are cheap to notice and costly to test, so one ungrounded hunch beside real themes poisons the rung that feeds every claim.
This page states what the Insight delivery holds and where the line between theme and hunch runs.

**The ladder and its letters**: The venue-free evidence ladder runs 1a-descriptions, 1b-themes, 1c-claims, 1d-advice, and this board reads it as Data, Insight, Claims, Design, echoing D, I, K, W.
Rung 1b is the step where described data becomes named patterns: the I.
A D id such as `D3` is one anchored entry in the 1a data profile; a T id such as `T1` is one theme here, and rung 1c later cites it as `C1 (T1)`.

**Where this page sits**: QB2 Data owns the anchored profile and the D ids a theme cites; QB4 Claims owns the adjudicated ledger, so a claim's status never appears on this rung.
This page owns the stretch between them: how a described fact becomes a pattern worth committing a claim on.
QB1 Opening carries the seed hypothesis the counter-hunt sweeps against, and it owns which venue an intervention picks.

**Why it matters**: This rung is where breadth is won or lost.
The three lenses make the sweep systematic instead of mood-dependent, and the Parked reservoir keeps every dropped pattern recoverable, so nothing described is silently thrown away.

**Covered elsewhere**: The probe mechanics that bind a theme's field questions to the bank belong to the evidence wall, QA4; this page says only that discovery is the lane this rung leans on.
The venue never touches this rung: the pattern space does not change with the channel, and the venue packs are the QBv pages.

### 2 · Grounded, or parked

**The grounding law**: extraction first, naming second.

```text
  🧩 theme T<n>   cites ≥1 resolving D id or discovery source
  💭 hunch        cites nothing ──▶ 🅿️ Parked, never listed as a theme
  📊 every D id   cited by ≥1 theme, or parked context-only with a why
```

🧩 Establishes the line the Opening promised: grounding is what separates a theme from a hunch.

A theme is extracted, not invented: every `T<n>` cites at least one `D<n>` id from the 1a profile or a discovery source, and that citation is the theme's footing, not decoration.
An ungrounded pattern is a hunch, and a hunch goes to Parked rather than into the theme list.

#### 2.1 · Discovery is this rung's lane into the bank
(1a leans on task probes in the data; 1b characteristically asks the field)
The questions this rung raises are field-shaped: does the literature name this pattern, and what does it call it?
They are raised as `Q-Theme` blocks in the rung artifact, filed in `1-probes/`, and bound to discovery answers; a quick in-data confirmation may still route to task.
The rung receives grounding and never produces it inline, so each answered question lands its references onto the T entry it grounds.

#### 2.2 · Full D-consumption
(nothing described is silently dropped)
Every 1a D id ends up cited by at least one theme or listed in Parked as context-only, with a why.
That is what makes the rung auditable: a D id that appears nowhere is a hole a checker can name.

### 3 · The sweep and the reservoir

**Three lenses, one bank**: how breadth survives the round.

```text
  🔍 lens 1    data patterns     what the D entries themselves suggest
  🔍 lens 2    field / lit       what discovery says the field names
  🔍 lens 3    counter-hunt      patterns AGAINST the seed hypothesis
  ⚖️ rule      each lens swept, or waived on record
  🅿️ bank      Parked holds what a round notices and drops
  🔁 re-mine   the next DRAFT re-reads Parked against new D ids
```

🔍 Establishes why the sweep is systematic and where a dropped pattern waits.

DRAFT sweeps three lenses: data patterns from the D entries, field patterns from discovery, and the counter-hunt.
Every lens is swept or waived on record, and the counter-hunt is the one whose empty result is still written down: a round that found nothing against the seed says so in Parked.
Parked is a reservoir, not a graveyard: the next round's DRAFT re-mines it first, asking whether new D entries now ground anything that was parked.

#### 3.1 · Why the counter-hunt earns its mandate
(the seed hypothesis is the intervention's reason to exist, which is exactly why it needs hunting)
QB1's seed states why the intervention should work, and every later rung inherits an incentive to confirm it.
The counter-hunt is the ladder's built-in correction: one lens whose whole job is to find the patterns that would embarrass the seed, run while finding them is still cheap.

### 4 · Promotion: the hook rung 1c consumes

**From pattern to claim**: what a hook is and when it may move.

```text
  🧩 theme       a question-space: direction + grounding
  🪝 hook        one candidate-claim sentence the theme carries
  ⚖️ 1c intake   commits a hook as C<n> (T<n>) and adjudicates it
  🚦 promotion   ready when a probe could REFUTE the hook
  🚫 never here  supported · weak · GAP · status lives only in 1c
```

🪜 Establishes the handoff: themes stay questions, and refute-capability is the promotion test.

A theme is a question-space, not a claim: it carries direction and candidate-claim hooks, and a claim's status lives only in the 1c ledger.
The promotion test is refute-capability: a hook is ready for rung 1c when it can be probed in a way that could prove it wrong.
A hook no probe could refute can only ever be confirmed, and the ledger would inherit an unfalsifiable claim.
A theme with no hook is legal when it says so: an explicit context-only note keeps it as background rather than a stalled promotion.

#### 4.1 · Where the test is enforced is still open
(the shipping gate never asks refutability, so the enforcement point needs a ruling)
The shipping 1b CHECK verifies grounding and hooks but never asks whether a hook could be refuted.
The Decision Now row in States puts that choice, 1b exit or 1c intake, in front of JL.

## Glossary

- 🧩 **Theme (`T<n>`)**: a named pattern extracted from described data or the field; a question-space worth testing, never a claim.
- 📊 **D id (`D<n>`)**: one anchored entry in the 1a data profile; the unit a theme's grounding cites.
- 🪝 **Hook**: the candidate-claim sentence a theme carries; exactly what rung 1c consumes when it commits a claim.
- 🅿️ **Parked**: the rung's reservoir: patterns noticed but not pursued, context-only D ids, and the counter-hunt record, re-mined at the next DRAFT.
- 🔍 **Counter-hunt**: the lens that sweeps for patterns against the seed hypothesis; it is swept or waived on record, and an empty result is written into Parked.
- 🚦 **Refute-capability**: the promotion test: a hook is ready for rung 1c when a probe could prove it wrong.
- 🏦 **The bank**: the project-level task and discovery layers where evidence actually runs; the rung asks it questions through `1-probes/` and never runs bank work inline.
- 🌱 **Seed hypothesis**: the 0-seed statement of why the intervention should work; what the counter-hunt sweeps against.
