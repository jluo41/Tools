## Content
### 1 · The two records do different things, and neither does both
#### `⑥` is approved and frozen; `⑤` is live and uncited
(one has authority and no update path, the other has an update path and no authority)
`⑥` carries numbered rulings that other skills quote by number, which is why it is durable: a changelog entry saying "R9" is checkable against a file that has an R9.
It also cannot answer a question raised today, cannot record who objected, and cannot close.
`⑤` can do all of that and has no standing, because nothing outside it has ever named it.

#### The pointer inside the constitution is the one that matters most
(`SKILL.md` line 19: "Spec and rationale (why it is built this way)")
The shared model itself sends a reader to `⑥`, twice in the file and once in its metadata summary.
So the layer's own constitution names a different folder as the reason it is built this way, and that folder does not know this board exists.
Anyone following the documentation faithfully arrives at 43 KB of frozen `.txt` and stops there.

### 2 · Graduation is what would connect them, and it has not started
#### One face is ✅ and zero Laws have been copied out
(the rule is that a settled ruling leaves the board for the owning skill)
The board's own close condition requires that `SKILL.md` says what these pages say, which is graduation by another name.
Nothing has graduated yet, which is defensible at 1 settled face out of 17 and will stop being defensible quickly.
Until something graduates, `⑤` is a record of arguments and `⑥` is the record of decisions, and the citation count is simply describing that accurately.

### 3 · The three ways out
```
 A  move the citations       ⑤ becomes the spec of record; ⑥ is
                             archived as the 260714 decisions.
                             cost: 10+ edits across 8 families.

 B  split by date            ⑥ owns what was decided 260714 (R1-R18);
                             ⑤ owns everything after. both cited,
                             each for its period. cost: one sentence
                             in both, and a reader who must check two.

 C  leave it                 ⑥ stays the contract; ⑤ is a working
                             record that graduates INTO the skills
                             and is never cited directly.
                             cost: none today, and this page says so.
```
`C` is the current state by default rather than by decision, which is the only reason this face is open.
`B` is the honest one if the R-numbers are load-bearing, because eight changelogs quote them and rewriting those entries would falsify history.
