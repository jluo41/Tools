#### 3.2 · The six moves, written as slots

#### 3.3 · What this desk refuses in an abstract
(from `abstract/style.md` "Anti-patterns" lines 71-79)
Labelled Background, Methods and Results fields are refused: all 5 published abstracts are single unstructured paragraphs. A "This paper studies" opener is refused whenever a phenomenon opener is available. Hiding the method is refused, which inverts the MISQ norm outright. A finding with no direction and no magnitude is refused. H1 and H2 labels are refused, as is IS-insider vocabulary such as TAM or UTAUT, because the readers here are economists, marketers and OR researchers. Ending on the technique is refused; the last sentence is the managerial insight or the robustness scope.
