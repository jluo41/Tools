## Opening

ISR and MISQ share a family, a section tree, and a refusal of method-as-contribution. They differ on one axis, and it is the axis a health-data paper is most exposed on. What does ISR want that MISQ does not?

**Where this page sits**: it is one outlet under `QBv1`, which owns what the UTD-IS family shares.
This page owns only what is true of `playbook-utd-is/ISR/`.

**Why it matters that this is a per-outlet difference**: the same result can be honest at MISQ and desk-rejected at ISR, purely on how the Discussion is worded.
`QBv1a` records the mirror of this: an associational posture is acceptable at MISQ when it is stated as one.

**What is unenforced**: layered robustness is a named desk signal here, and nothing in the paper lifecycle checks whether a submission has it.

## Content

### 1 · The one axis that separates ISR from MISQ

**Identification, and how the Discussion is allowed to talk**: same family, same sections, different bar.

```text
  🏛 MISQ   associational with strong controls is FINE
            ── if stated clearly

  🔬 ISR    identification rewarded MORE
            ── and OLS plus "causes" in the Discussion is a
               desk-reject, not a revision request

  💥 so ONE result, two venues, two different Discussions
     and the choice is made at VENUE, never at revise
```

🎯 Establishes the difference as a wording consequence with a venue cause, which is why it cannot be fixed downstream.

#### 1.1 · The gap sentence has to be genuinely empty
(the pack calls out the phrasing, which means the desk has seen it misused)
ISR's signals name the "one of the first studies to" formulation and require the gap to be actually empty rather than under-studied.
An overstated gap sentence is cheap to write and expensive to defend at this desk.

### 2 · What arriving here costs

**The theory section, plus an identification strategy that has to already exist**: one is structural, the other is not acquirable.


## Law

ISR rewards identification more than MISQ does, so one result carries two different Discussions at the two desks, and the posture is chosen at venue and never repaired at revise.
An identification strategy is a study-design property, so this outlet is decided upstream of the venue stage.

## Glossary

- **Layered robustness**: several named identification checks reported individually, which this desk lists as a fit signal rather than an appendix courtesy.
- **Posture**: how strongly a Discussion is permitted to speak about causation at a given desk.
