## Opening

MISQ, ISR, MS-IS, and MS-Marketing all refuse a paper whose contribution is a method. Past that agreement, what actually separates them?

MISQ takes any method if the theory is deep enough. ISR wants the hypothesis chain tight and the identification strong. MS-IS wants an economic mechanism, formal or credibly identified. MS-Marketing wants a consumer or firm decision with a welfare consequence.

**Where this page sits**: it is one pack under `QBv0`, which owns what any pack owes.
This page owns only what is true of `playbook-utd-is` and its four outlets, and it points at the pack rather than restating its tables.

**Why this pack matters most to this repo**: it is the lane `ProjB-PhyTrait-OpioidRx` targets, and it is the only pack whose README carries a full lean-signal matrix rather than prose.
That matrix is the sharpest routing instrument in the whole venue tree, and nothing outside the pack reads it yet.

**What is unusual in this pack's shape**: every outlet carries a `<outlet>-theory/` section folder, which no other pack in the tree has.
Theory is a first-class manuscript section here, not a part of the Introduction, and a paper retargeted into this pack from JAMA or Nature acquires a section it never had.

## Content

### 1 · What the four desks actually disagree about

**Not rigor, and not scale**: the disagreement is about what a theory is allowed to rest on.

```text
  MISQ   ── one primary theory used rigorously
            pluralistic method: survey · archival · interpretive
            · design science
            💥 desk-reject: a better classifier

  ISR    ── a named mechanism per hypothesis
            quantitative: DiD / IV / RD / matching · experiments
            💥 desk-reject: a loose theory-to-hypothesis chain

  MS-IS  ── an economic micro-foundation or a formal model
            reduced form only when identification is credible
            💥 desk-reject: mechanism asserted, not modeled

  MS-Mkt ── behavioral or economic interpretation, required
            💥 desk-reject: pure prediction with no behavioral
               interpretation
```

🎯 Establishes the routing question as "what does the theory rest on", which the pack's lean-signal matrix answers row by row.

#### 1.1 · The matrix routes on signals a paper already knows about itself
(so venue choice here needs no new evidence, only an honest reading)
`README.md` lists eight signals, among them *theory-forward and pluralistic*, *causal identification with an economics framing*, and *computational methods on large-scale data*.
Two of the eight point at more than one outlet, which is the pack telling the truth: those signals do not decide, and something else has to.

### 2 · What this pack changes downstream

**Four stage maps, and one of them adds a manuscript section**: retargeting into this pack is not a rewrite of tone.

```text
  ->Claims     a claim must name its theoretical mechanism
  ->Display    theory-forward outlets spend floats on mechanism,
               not on cohort description
  ->Minimap    the arc runs theory ━▶ hypotheses ━▶ test
  ->Write/Edit the language profile in style-profile.md

  🧩 AND the structural delta the other packs never impose:
     <outlet>-theory/  is its own section
     ── a paper arriving from JAMA or Nature has no such section
        and cannot produce one by rewriting its Introduction
```

🔁 Establishes that a retarget into UTD-IS is a structural change, which is why QB1 files venue with seed rather than after it.

#### 2.1 · The theory section is the retarget cost nothing else measures
(it matters because word budgets travel and sections do not)
Every other pack in the tree maps abstract, introduction, methods, results, discussion, appendix.
This pack adds a seventh, and no `style.md` anywhere else has a source for it.
