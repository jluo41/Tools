#### 3.1 · What the primary claim is at each desk
(the one row of the delta table that decides the outlet)
At MISQ the primary claim is the THEORETICAL contribution: a new construct, a specified mechanism, a resolved theoretical tension, or abstracted design principles [playbook-utd-is/README.md, Claims per-journal note].
At ISR it is a tight theory to hypotheses to evidence chain, or the first large-scale causal evidence that an IT artifact or policy moves an outcome.
At MS-IS it is the economic or analytical result: a characterized equilibrium, an identified causal effect, or a recovered structural primitive, usually carrying a welfare statement rather than firm profit alone.
At MS-Marketing it is causal or structural evidence on a marketing decision, with the welfare or profit consequence stated.
The theory bar is highest at MISQ and the method is the most pluralistic: survey, archival, qualitative or interpretive, and design science all pass [playbook-utd-is/README.md, Outlets delta].

#### 4.1 · The moves, as slots
(fill the shape; never lift the exemplar's sentence)
Open on the question: `[This work] examines the question of [who or what] in the context of [setting], by [theory] and [analytics].` [move 1, Liu 2021 / Gao 2015 / Yin 2014].
Subordinate the method: `It [trains] [the model] to infer [the construct]. This enables analyses to reveal [the role of the construct in the outcome].` [move 2, Liu 2021].
Name constructs and directions in the finding: `higher [outcome] is related to higher [c1], [c2], [c3] and to lower [c4].` [move 4, Liu 2021].
Close forward: `Theoretical and practical implications are discussed.` or `This study is the first to provide empirical evidence of [relationship], and extends [literature] to [domain].` [move 3, Liu 2021 / Gao 2015].
Healthcare and policy papers may carry the number when the number IS the finding: `when [driver] increases from [a] to [b], [outcome] falls from [p] to [q].` [move 7, Ayabakan 2025 / Zhang 2025].
Flag a surprise: `contrary to common assumptions, [subjects] perceived [A] as more [trait] than [B], even though [the interactions were identical].` [move 8, Raimi 2025].
Compress a study sequence into one sentence: `We conducted [k] experiments and a qualitative study.` [move 9, Raimi 2025].
The design science variant swaps the arc for problem, artifact NAME, design principles, evaluation, contribution: `we propose [ARTIFACT] to automate [the task] using minimal training data.` [move 10, Liu-EBM 2025].
