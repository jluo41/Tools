## Opening

MISQ is the most method-permissive desk in this tree and the least contribution-permissive. Survey, archival, qualitative, interpretive, design science: all fine. A better classifier: not a paper. So what is it actually buying?

**Where this page sits**: it is one outlet under `QBv1`, which owns what the UTD-IS family shares.
This page owns only what is true of `playbook-utd-is/MISQ/`.

**Why this outlet matters to this repo**: it is the venue `ProjB-PhyTrait-OpioidRx` targets, so its one-sentence test is the bar this repo's IS work is actually scored on.

**What is unread**: the test is a sentence in `taste.md` and nothing in the paper lifecycle applies it.

## Content

### 1 · What the desk buys, and what it will not

**One primary theory used rigorously**: not three or four stacked loosely.

```text
  📐 the shape MISQ rewards
     ① ONE primary theory, used rigorously
     ② a specified MECHANISM, not a measured association
     ③ large-scale archival or field data testing it cleanly
     ④ honest identification posture ── associational with
        strong controls is fine IF STATED

  💥 the failure mode nearest this repo:
     "clinical epidemiology that happens to use health IT data
      but has no IS question"
     ── a physician-behavior paper can land here by accident
```

🎯 Establishes the desk's demand as a specified mechanism, and names the failure mode a health-data paper is most likely to hit.

#### 1.1 · Honest associational framing is accepted here and punished elsewhere
(so the identification posture is a venue decision, not a methods one)
MISQ accepts an associational claim stated as one; ISR rewards identification more.
The same result therefore needs different Discussion language at the two desks, and the choice is made at venue, not at revision.

### 2 · What arriving here costs

**A theory section, and a change of what the method is for**: neither is a rewrite of tone.

```text
  🧩 GAINS  MISQ-theory/ ── a first-class section
            no JAMA, Nature, PNAS, or Diabetes Care
            outlet has one

  🔄 CHANGES  the method moves OUT of the contribution
              and INTO the enabler role
              ── an ML paper does not get retargeted here,
                 it gets re-argued

  📚 15 exemplars behind the section norms
```

🧩 Establishes the arrival cost as structural plus argumentative, which is why a retarget into MISQ is decided early.

## Law

At MISQ the method enables the contribution and is never it, so a paper whose claim is its technique has no route to this desk that does not re-argue the claim.
An associational posture is acceptable here only when it is stated as one.

## Glossary

- **Enabler role**: the position a method occupies at this desk, described in Methods and excluded from the contribution.
