- [Submission guidelines](https://www.nature.com/nathumbehav/submission-guidelines) · the per-journal door, which routes to two pages that matter here. [Formatting your initial submission](https://www.nature.com/nathumbehav/submission-guidelines/initial-formatting) governs what is sent first: "We accept initial submissions in PDF, Word or TeX/LaTeX formats; if you are using TeX/LaTeX, please submit compiled PDFs." [AIP and formatting](https://www.nature.com/nathumbehav/submission-guidelines/aip-and-formatting) governs the accepted manuscript: "Please submit your manuscript in either Word or TeX/LaTeX format. We do not accept PDFs for final submissions." That second page is portfolio boilerplate served per journal: it is about 98% identical to the same page at Nature Medicine and Nature Machine Intelligence once the journal name is substituted, so its rules bind all three.
- [Content types](https://www.nature.com/nathumbehav/content) · per journal, and the only place the limits differ from the siblings. An Article takes a main text of up to 5,000 words excluding abstract, Methods, references and figure legends, an abstract of up to 150 words and unreferenced, and up to 8 display items (figures and/or tables), which is the widest display allowance of the four Nature-branded outlets in this group. On references it is the outlier that grants most: "we do not place limits on reference lists." There is no Brief Communication here; the primary-research types are Article, Registered Report and Resource, and all three carry the same limits and the same four-block division.
- [Springer Nature LaTeX author support](https://www.springernature.com/gp/authors/campaigns/latex-author-support) · the published LaTeX package, and this journal sends authors to exactly this address, saying they may go there to "download the Springer Nature LaTeX template". A journal-specific class file does not exist; the instruction is "To submit a TeX/LaTeX file, please use any of the standard class files such as article.cls, revtex.cls or amsart.cls." The template itself is portfolio-wide and wider, usable "for any Springer Nature journal inclusive of Springer, Nature Portfolio, and BMC".
- Tiers, and both claims this board makes · the 10-item Extended Data cap is CONFIRMED at source, which settles the hole this page's own Log records, that the cap was inherited from the NMI and Nature Medicine appendix guides and restated by no NHB exemplar. The journal's line is "A maximum of 10 Extended Data display figures is permitted." The unit is display FIGURES, not items, and the tier below it is subordinate to it, since "Supplementary Figures should be used only for cases when the use of Extended Data to report these findings is not appropriate." The no-standalone-Related-Work rule is CONFIRMED too, from an exhaustive list rather than a prohibition: "Article should be divided as follows: Introduction (without heading), Results (concise, focused account of the findings), Discussion, Methods", followed by "Results and Methods should be divided by topical subheadings; the Discussion does not contain subheadings." Four blocks are permitted, none of them is prior work, and the two that host it may carry no heading of their own.

## Glossary

- **WEIRD**: Western, educated, industrialized, rich, democratic; the sample composition this desk refuses to see presented as a universal claim.
- **Claim-scope error**: overstating the population a finding covers, fixable at revision, as against a design error, which is not.
