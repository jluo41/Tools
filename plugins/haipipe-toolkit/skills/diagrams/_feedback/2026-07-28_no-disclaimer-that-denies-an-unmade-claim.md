JL dropped "Associational." from the theory figure's caption.

The reasoning is worth keeping: a theory figure reports no estimate, so there is nothing to
mislabel, and the caption uses no causal verb anywhere, so the disclaimer was denying a claim the
caption never made. What does the honest-label work is the wording itself ("is positively
associated with") plus the H1/H2/H3 tags, which mark the arrows as predictions rather than findings.
The venue's associational boundary is a Results and Discussion obligation, discharged by §6 and §7.

The rule: a disclaimer must deny a claim the artifact actually makes. A boilerplate one added
because the venue contract mentions the word is noise, and it teaches a reader that the labels are
decorative.
