partly no: "I don't know, for these issues, why you cannot solve it? you should use
/haipipe-paper-probe to do it, right?"

I had spent most of the session editing S pages, probe entries and tex by hand and had never invoked
the PROBE phase at all. Hand-work looks like progress and cannot see what the phase's checker sees.

One invocation found, in a single run: `S-Main-5` carried exactly ONE recognizable Q-consumer record
and it was already answered, so the checker read the page as green while three evidence-shaped
questions sat in prose items with no ids and no entries; a `\cite{TOADD}` that named no owning
question; and EIGHT harvest debts elsewhere in the paper.

None of those would have surfaced from reading. They surfaced from running the checker.

The rule: when a phase has a skill, invoke it before hand-working the same ground. Reading a page
tells you what it says; running its checker tells you what it fails.
