citations".

This lifts the "agents never write bibtex" rule that two S pages carried, and replaces a question to
the human with a check-list for the human.

The discipline that makes the grant safe is to separate TRANSCRIPTION from GUESS, per entry, IN the
`.bib` itself rather than only in the reply. Of three entries written: two were transcribed from
Crossref-verified identifiers already sitting in the bank, and the third, `stock2005testing`, was not
bank-verified and was written from the citing sentence's own quoted value. The `.bib` comment block
says which is which and says to check that one first.

Result: zero broken citation keys in the compiled manuscript; every remaining undefined citation is a
deliberate `TOADD` rather than a dangling key.

The distinction to preserve: a PLACED citation is not a BANKED answer. Both probe entries stayed open
after their citations were placed, because the bank's digest is still owed and LAW 1 keeps writing it
out of the consumer's hands. Closing the entry because the prose now compiles would have laundered a
missing digest into a green check.
