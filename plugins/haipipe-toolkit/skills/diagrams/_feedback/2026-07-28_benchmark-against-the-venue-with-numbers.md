JL, verbatim: "How many words here, and compare to other MISQ paper, will this be sufficent?"

The question was answerable by measurement, not judgment, and the measurement changed the answer.
§3 runs ~1,130 prose words against a ~2,900 floor. §2's six donor paragraphs measure exactly 1,000
words, so after the ruled fold §3 reaches ~2,130, about 73 percent of the floor, still 7 to 9
paragraphs short of the 20 to 22 the exemplars carry. The fold also does NOT fix citation density
and slightly dilutes it, because only 19 of §2's keys are new to §3.

The blueprint's own subsection recipe then names the fix precisely: §3 runs about 2.3 paragraphs per
hypothesis where the recipe wants 3 to 6.

The rule: when a row asks "is this enough", check first whether it is a MEASUREMENT nobody has taken
rather than a ruling nobody has made. Two of this session's long-open questions were measurements:
this one, and the display wiring gap.
