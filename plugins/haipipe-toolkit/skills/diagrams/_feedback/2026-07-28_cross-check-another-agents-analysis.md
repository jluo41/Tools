JL pasted a long analysis from another session about the headache fork and asked "How do you
think about this."

It contained one genuinely important catch (our own `marmura2015acute` grades butorphanol Level A,
so "opioids do not work for headache" is refuted by the paper's own citation) and four problems:
a units error putting metformin's linear-probability coefficient in a column of MME coefficients,
a missing cohort (osteoarthritis, the rung that proves the construct needs both halves), NSAIDs
counted as mechanism-targeted for migraine but as a mere preference order for low back pain, and
a too-quick resolution of the cancer null.

I also doubted one of its figures (metformin p = 0.512) and was WRONG: it is verified in
tasks/Z02_Display_PhyTraitDiabetes/QA/1-metformin-null.md. So the rule cuts both ways.

The rule: verify before relaying and before doubting. Check the claim against disk, then say which
parts survived.
