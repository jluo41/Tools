## Opening

How do Paper and Writing compose a reviewed manuscript while preserving human decisions and release ownership?

**Where this Page sits:** Paper consumes accepted evidence and Ideation handoffs, then routes work through its own Pages and gates.

**Why it matters:** Drafting, human decisions, evidence readiness, and release remain separately reviewable.

## Content

### 1 · Paper and writing owners
**Scope**: Paper routes composition; Writing works within the host's scope.
```text
evidence + direction ──▶ Paper plan ──▶ human-AI Writing Run
                                              │
                                              ▼
accepted content ──▶ Page CHECK ──▶ Paper release
```

#### 1.1 · Family inventory
(Open the Paper router and the common Writing contract.)
The Paper family contains 9 `SKILL.md` files under `paper-family`. `haipipe-paper` routes Page Types and workflow owners. The shared Writing capability is `haipipe-writing`.

#### 1.2 · Preserve Paper decisions
(Keep venue and release authority with Paper.)
Paper owns Story, Venue, Section, Round, and its G0-G5 human decision gates. A Page Structure Run, Page CHECK, and Paper release decision are distinct records. Paper gates are not extra Runs unless the Paper owner commissions bounded Runs for them.

#### 1.3 · Human-AI co-work
**Scope**: shared structure, scoped writing feedback, human acceptance.

#### 1.4 · Co-develop the Page structure
(Use one shared Run with recorded participants and contributors.)
`rp-struct-01` combines SHAPE and SURVEY for one Page. A person and agent may contribute Steps to the same Run. The person closes its structure before the workflow proposes Section or paragraph writing Runs. See the `page-writing-run`.

#### 1.5 · Keep writing scope fixed
(Each Section or paragraph Run names the exact target.)
One Section Run or fixed paragraph Run contains its candidate, human review, diagnosis, revision, and acceptance record. A prose cycle is a Step within that Run. A new Run needs an independent goal or materially changed scope.

#### 1.6 · Separate writing from evidence and release
(Human acceptance applies to the selected text, not the whole Page.)
Task and Discovery Runs supply evidence under their own owners. Page Content adopts accepted wording after planned Page Runs and required Results are ready; CHECK judges the exact delivered Page. Paper's venue and release gates remain separate.
