# Evidence into ideas
state: 🟡 PARTIAL
owner: JL

## Opening

How does Ideation turn accepted evidence into a research direction and Paper handoff?

**Where this Page sits:** Ideation is a semantic bridge between evidence-producing Runs and research composition.

**Why it matters:** A research idea stays connected to the evidence that motivated it and to the Page or Paper work that uses it.

## Content

### 1 · Ideation ownership
**Scope**: the ideation owner synthesizes directions from accepted evidence.
```text
accepted evidence ──▶ candidate directions
                              │
                              ▼
human selection ──▶ Paper handoff
```

#### 1.1 · Family inventory
(Use Ideation for evidence bundles and research directions.)
The Ideation family contains 8 `SKILL.md` files under `ideation-family`. `haipipe-ideation` owns the entry point and the handoff to Paper.

#### 1.2 · Evidence inputs
(Carry source Run identities into the evidence bundle.)
Ideation can use accepted Task and Discovery Results and relevant Insight outputs. The bundle records provenance so a proposed direction remains traceable to its supporting evidence.

#### 1.3 · Paper handoff
**Scope**: Ideation proposes direction; Paper owns composition.

#### 1.4 · Keep the writing boundary
(Pass the accepted idea into Paper without taking over its Page.)
Ideation proposes research directions and a Paper handoff. Paper owns its Page Types, Story, Venue, Section, Round, review decisions, and release gates.

#### 1.5 · Workflow and Runs
(Use the current Run Specs for each bounded ideation commission.)
The Ideation owner defines its Run list, dependencies, evidence inputs, and Result shape. This Page does not turn labels such as bundle, ideas, or handoff into Runs unless the owner declares them as Run Specs.
