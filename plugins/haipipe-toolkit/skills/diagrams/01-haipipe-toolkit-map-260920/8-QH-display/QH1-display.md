# Display and visual output
state: 🟡 PARTIAL
owner: JL

## Opening

How do Display skills turn accepted inputs into clear visual artifacts while preserving evidence ownership?

**Where this Page sits:** Display can receive material from Page, Paper, Design, and evidence-producing Jobs.

**Why it matters:** A visual remains traceable to the claims and evidence it presents.

## Content

### 1 · Display ownership
**Scope**: formatting and visual production do not replace evidence judgment.
```text
accepted input ──▶ render / assemble Run ──▶ visual artifact
                                                    │
                                                    ▼
                                           owning Page or Paper
```

#### 1.1 · Family inventory
(Route the requested artifact to the matching visual skill.)
The Display family contains 14 `SKILL.md` files under `display-family`. It covers figures, tables, diagrams, presentations, posters, and related renderers.

#### 1.2 · Preserve claim ownership
(The source Page or Paper remains responsible for meaning and evidence.)
Display formats or assembles accepted material. A visual renderer does not validate the truth of a claim, decide evidence sufficiency, or grant release on behalf of Page, Paper, Insight, or Design.

#### 1.3 · Workflow and Runs
**Scope**: the owner declares the bounded visual operation and output.

#### 1.4 · Bind inputs and outputs
(Keep the source, rendering method, and resulting artifact addressable.)
The selected Display skill defines its inputs, output format, quality checks, and any Run contract. The Workflow lists those bounded Runs and their dependencies; the Board does not invent a universal Display sequence.

#### 1.5 · Handoff
(Return the artifact to its consumer with source links.)
Return figures and rendered files with the Page, Paper, or Design source that owns their interpretation. The consumer records the accepted artifact and any final delivery decision.
