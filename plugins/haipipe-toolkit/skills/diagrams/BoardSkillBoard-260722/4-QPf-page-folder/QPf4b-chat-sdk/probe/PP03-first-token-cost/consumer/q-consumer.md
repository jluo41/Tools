8.1s to the first token and a near-$0.9 full-tier message, both attributed to
connecting again and loading the ~150-skill list once per message. Three numbers,
no run, no script, no date, and no after-figure at all: the page asserts the fix
worked without ever printing what it costs now.

again, and costs a small fraction of the first". A fraction is a RATIO, so the
row cannot be ticked without a before and an after measured the same way. P1
