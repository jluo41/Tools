One primary file a person rules, one derived view

      │        keys in fences or backticks are
      ▼        illustrations, stripped before the scan
🗃 bibex/⟨stem⟩.bib       PRIMARY · committed · refresh
      ▲ seeded whole from   only APPENDS, never overwrites
      │ the paper's 0-*.bib  or deletes what is already here
      ▼
🖼 bibex/⟨stem⟩-bib.html  DERIVED · regenerated freely

An entry no longer cited stays, marked "not cited on this page" — removing a sentence never silently drops its source.

Two files, two owners. The .bib is the person's; a rebuild may only append to it, because a person may have edited any entry. The HTML view is the machine's and gets thrown away freely. And note the fence-strip: a figure showing cite syntax is an illustration, not a citation.

Three doors, one truth

Seeding copies in, a person ticks and pastes, the view renders

↻ /_board/bibex

Re-scan the page's keys, seed-import what resolves, re-render the view.

✓ /_board/bibex-verify

Writes verified = {WHO YYMMDD} into the entry itself — no sidecar to drift.

✎ /_board/bibex-entry

Lands a person's pasted bibtex verbatim, duplicate-guarded — the pen, never the author.

The view's own buttons POST the verify and entry routes and reload; a lit-click on the tab refreshes the derived half only.

Three routes, and each one has a different relationship to the truth file. Refresh may only add. Verify writes one field, on a person's click. Entry lands text a person wrote. Nothing in this list composes a citation — that's the whole design.

The workbench · what a person does on a card

Check, mark, fix, add

  • 🔎 Scholar, 🔗 DOI, 📄 URL — the links the checking happens through
  • ✅ checked with who and when, or ⬜ unchecked with the ✓ button — and undo
  • ▸ raw bibtex behind an ✎ edit fold — fix a field, replace in place
  • 🔴 missing key → 🔎 find it → 📋 paste its bibtex → it lands verbatim
  • ＋ add takes a LINK too: DOI, arXiv, Scholar's cite link, or a paper URL — the bibtex is fetched whole into the box for review
  • The whole raw .bib sits one fold from the cards, path printed — hand-editing it is legal
Walk one card slowly: parsed title, authors, year, then the three links, then the status. The ＋ box is the newest part — paste a paper link and four resolvers fetch the bibtex whole into the box, so fetching is copying and landing stays the person's second click. An unusable fetched key gets renamed surname-plus-year, because a key is a local handle, not metadata.

The law

The pen, never the author

🗃 The page owns its bib

Nothing this plugin does ever writes the paper's 0-*.bib.

✂️ Verbatim or whole

The machine lands pasted text or copies an upstream entry; a missing key is reported, never guessed.

🧑 The ✓ is a person's

No machine may write verified — the one demo tick that did was reverted the same hour.

The pen validates shape only — one balanced entry with a key, duplicates refused unless the save is an explicit replace.

This is the slide to slow down on. The line between a pen and an author is that the pen never composes, completes, or corrects the text. And the ✓ is the page's answer to "did I check this link myself" — we reverted a machine-written tick within the hour precisely to keep that field honest.
