The Law · each line signed by JL, 260815

Six rulings the surface obeys

  • 💬 One Chat per page — the form is selected after opening, inside the surface.
  • 🔓 The form split is never a permission split — both carry the full tier.
  • 📂 A kept conversation is plugin material at <page>/chat/, in the meeting shape.
  • 📄 What lands: the transcript, derived from the session's jsonl; the digest stays a later aim.
  • 🔔 When: drawer close and session switch — registered sessions only, never every stray conversation.
  • ✍️ Who writes: keep_sessions in live/chat.py behind POST /_board/chat-keep; a re-keep overwrites, jsonl stays the source.
This is the page's spine — every line traces to a dated ruling, mostly from one evening. The bound on "when" matters most in practice: only sessions the page has registered land, at the moments a conversation stops being on screen. Nothing is hoovered up.
