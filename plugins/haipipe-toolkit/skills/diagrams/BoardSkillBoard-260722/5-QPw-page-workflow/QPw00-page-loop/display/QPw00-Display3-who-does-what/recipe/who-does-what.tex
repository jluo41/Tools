0 & OPEN & you name the page and the one thing it must decide (\texttt{haipipe-page} CREATE, not a phase) &

\band{the loop --- CHECK may send you back to any row in this band}
1 & OUTLINE & a machine writes the SHAPE only: sections, bullets, and what each
    bullet still owes &
    \you approve it, or send it back \newline \cost{reject costs 10 seconds; this
    is the cheapest gate} & 10 min & \loopy each round\\

2 & DRAFT & a machine turns each approved bullet into sentences carrying visible
    \texttt{<HOLE>}s &
    nothing. \newline Do NOT fill a hole & 0 & \loopy each round\\

3 & PROBE & a machine looks for an answer that already exists, then opens a card
    and asks the bank &
    nothing & 0 & \loopy each round\\

4c & CITATION & a PERSON finds the published work; a machine may copy bibtex and
    may never compose it &
    \you paste the entry, tick \texttt{verified} & 20 min & \loopy per entry\\

4v & VALUE & the bank answers, bound BY PATH to a real file, with the proof
    pulled next to the card &
    \you read the proof, tick \texttt{read:} \newline \cost{this is the cost, and
    the tick is what permits quoting} & 30 min & \loopy per card\\

4d & DISPLAY & a machine freezes the exact bytes a figure will be drawn from,
    and never draws &
    nothing & 0 & \loopy per figure\\

\multicolumn{6}{@{}l}{\footnotesize\itshape\quad 4c, 4v and 4d run at the SAME
    TIME. None of the three waits for another to finish.}\\

5 & REVISE & a machine replaces every landed hole, draws the figures, and
    rebuilds the pdf &
    nothing & 0 & \loopy each round\\

6 & CHECK & a machine and a DIFFERENT reader judge the BUILT pdf, never the
    markdown, and never the producer's own work &
    \you accept, or say where to go back \newline
    {\footnotesize$\hookrightarrow$ back to 1, 2, 3, 4 or 5} & 15 min &
    \loopy each round\\

\band{reached only when CHECK says CLOSE}
7 & ACCEPT & each figure is bound to the inputs it was accepted with &
    \you tick \texttt{accepted:} \newline \cost{it reverts if the inputs change}
    & 10 min & once per figure\\

8 & CLOSE & the page's own ruling, named by its Page Type &
    \you sign it \newline \cost{this ends the round} & 5 min & once per round\\
