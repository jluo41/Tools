Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about MIS Quarterly. Browse by category below.

Access & Subscriptions

Do you offer physical copies of MIS Quarterly?
▼

No. MISQ discontinued print editions in 2019 and is now fully digital.

How can my institution or library subscribe to MISQ?
▼

MISQ does not directly manage institutional subscriptions. All institutional subscriptions are handled by EBSCO Information Services. For more information, please contact [email&#160;protected] .

I purchased articles on the original MISQ website. How can I continue to access them?
▼

For access to previous purchases, email [email&#160;protected] and include the titles or products you're seeking.

Can I log in with my account from the old MISQ website?
▼

No. You will need to create a new user account for this website.

Publication

When will the First Look or Article in Advance version of my manuscript be published?
▼

We aim to publish First Look versions within one month of acceptance. Our copyeditor will reach out to the authors to coordinate final revisions before the Article in Advance version is published.

Things to consider:

Articles are published in order of acceptance date.

Delays may occur near quarterly issue deadlines.

For time-sensitive requests (e.g., tenure reviews), contact [email&#160;protected] and [email&#160;protected] .

Permissions

Can I reuse a figure from an MISQ article in my own work?
▼

Yes, but you need to request permission through the Copyright Clearance Center . This keeps a record in our system and provides you with digital access to the license.

Can my MISQ article be published as open access?
▼

Yes, MISQ articles can be published under the authors' preferred Creative Commons license . The fee for open access publication is $5,000 USD.

Editorial & Review Process

How can I become a reviewer for MIS Quarterly?
▼

You can attend a reviewer development workshop, typically held in January and promoted on our website and social media. You may also contact 1–2 associate editors in your field to express your interest in reviewing.

Advertising & Services
