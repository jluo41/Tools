ScholarOne Submission Guide

Complete guide to submitting your manuscript to MIS Quarterly

Follow these three essential steps to ensure your submission meets all MISQ requirements. All manuscripts must be submitted through our ScholarOne portal with proper transparency commitments and required submission materials.

🔑

Step 1
Portal Access & Submission

All manuscripts must be submitted through MISQ's ScholarOne Manuscripts portal. This is your gateway to the submission process and all future communications.

1

Access the ScholarOne Portal

All manuscripts must be submitted through MISQ's ScholarOne Manuscripts site. This is the only accepted submission method.

2

Communication Protocol

All communications regarding your manuscript and submission will take place through the ScholarOne portal. All authors must be copied on any digital exchanges with senior editors or MISQ staff.

Ready to Submit?

Access the ScholarOne Manuscripts portal to begin your submission process.

🌐
Access ScholarOne Portal

📊

Step 2
Research Transparency

All submissions must include transparency commitments to support open science and reproducible research practices.

1

Transparency Commitment Upload

Authors need to complete and upload a transparency commitment as part of their submission. This should be submitted during the "Step 2: Upload Files" section under Miscellaneous.

📁
Download Template

📌 Important Note

For more details regarding our transparency initiative, please visit our dedicated transparency page for comprehensive guidelines and requirements.

📋

Step 3
Follow ScholarOne Submission Requirements

In 2026, we have made changes to incorporate the required materials such as cover letter, editor nominations and others into the ScholarOne submission process. Therefore, it's essential our contributors pay extra attention to the new steps within submission. These include:

Complete Research Transparency Commitment (see step 2 of the submission)

Ensure you nominate Editors and Reviewers during the submission process.

⚠️ Pay Close Attention

Please ensure you complete all sections within ScholarOne to avoid submission delays. Remember an official Transparency Statement document is still required (Step 2).

Ready to Submit Your Manuscript?

Before submitting, make sure you have completed all three requirements:

✓
Access to ScholarOne Portal

✓
Transparency Commitment Completed

✓
ScholarOne Requirements Reviewed

🚀
Begin Your Submission
