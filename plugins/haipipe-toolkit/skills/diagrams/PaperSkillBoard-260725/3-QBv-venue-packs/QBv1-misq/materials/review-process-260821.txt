Our Commitment

Peer review at MIS Quarterly is rigorous and constructive. Reviews are focused on improving scholarship while maintaining the highest standards of quality and fairness.

Anonymous Review

MIS Quarterly uses a double‑anonymous process: authors and reviewers remain unknown to one another to support impartiality and fairness. Editors are aware of author identities to manage the process, but this information is not shared with reviewers.

Roles in the Process

Authors

Submit original research that advances the field. Respond constructively and thoroughly to editor and reviewer feedback.

Reviewers

Provide timely, rigorous, and developmental evaluations that add value and help authors maximize the potential of their work.

Associate Editors (AEs)

Coordinate peer review, synthesize reviewer input, and make a recommendation to the SE.

Senior Editors (SEs)

Make the final editorial decision for each round of review and ensure consistency, fairness, and quality of the process.

Transparency Editors (TEs)

Oversees research transparency requirements and expectations.

Editor-in-Chief (EIC)

Conducts preliminary review of each submission. Assigns submissions to senior editors for peer review.

Peer Review Flow

Manuscripts undergo an initial review by a Senior Editor and Associate Editor, followed by peer review from external reviewers. The Associate Editor and reviewer feedback helps guide the Senior Editor’s decision.

At a glance:

External reviewers: 0–3

Initial decision timeline: ~3–5 months

Fee to submit: $0

Outcomes of Review

Accepted Papers

Accepted manuscripts are first posted as Forthcoming manuscripts with a permanent DOI. They then proceed through copyediting and typesetting before they are assigned to an issue for publication.

Revisions

Many submissions undergo one or more rounds of revision based on editorial guidance and reviewer feedback. Authors recieve copies of reviewer feedback.

Rejections

Please review the journal's policy on submitting a previously rejected paper.
