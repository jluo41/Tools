## Aims · what must become true, and how you would know

Transcribed from the page's own `## Aims`; DRAFT keeps the page and this list identical.

- A1.1 · A study's sampled population is recorded where a venue is chosen.
  **Done when:** a paper pinned to this outlet states what population its claim covers.
- A2.1 · The mechanism demand is recognized as shared across MISQ, ISR, MS-IS, and this desk.
  **Done when:** a paper carrying a mechanism is shortlisted across both packs rather than one.
- A3.1 · A drafted abstract names its design and its sample scale in the pivot sentence.
  **Done when:** the sentence beginning "We" or "Here" carries N, countries, or preregistration, and the whole paragraph sits inside the 100-180 word band.
- A4.1 · The gap paragraph is written as a generalizability limit, not as a technical unknown.
  **Done when:** the gap sentence names the region, population, or sample-size ceiling of the prior evidence, and the contribution pivot carries the scale that answers it.
- A5.1 · The `related-work` unit emits placements rather than a manuscript section.
  **Done when:** each prior-work item is routed to Introduction, Discussion, Results, or Supplementary Information, and no section is written under that name.
- A5.2 · The seven NHB guides are re-mined against more than three exemplars.
  **Done when:** at least one `nhb-<section>/style.md` cites a paper other than cologna-2025, gachter-2025, or fillingim-2025.
- A6.1 · Methods documents the sampled population with its inclusion, exclusion, and exclusion rates.
  **Done when:** a participant subsection names who was eligible, who was dropped, and at what rate, including attention checks for any online panel.
- A6.2 · Pre-registration status is stated either way and never left implicit.
  **Done when:** Methods carries a registration reference with its deviations or the flat negative, plus a power analysis or a no-predetermination statement.
- A7.1 · Every primary effect carries its 95% CI and a between- or within-group breakdown.
  **Done when:** no primary finding stands as a single pooled number, and exploratory analyses are labelled where they appear.
- A8.1 · The scope limitation names the population in words and carries a concrete future direction.
  **Done when:** a limitation paragraph names the sampled population and pairs it with a cross-cultural or within-subject next step, and the section closes on significance.
- A9.1 · Display units are triaged into body, Extended Data, or Supplementary Information before the cap can be breached.
  **Done when:** the display plan counts Extended Data items, holds the total at 10 or fewer, and numbers every item in full with no "S" prefix.
