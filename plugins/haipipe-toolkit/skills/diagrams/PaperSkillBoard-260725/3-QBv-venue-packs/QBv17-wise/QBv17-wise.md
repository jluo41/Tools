## Opening

WISE is the Workshop on Information Systems and Economics, and its 37th edition runs in Lisbon, Portugal, December 16-18, 2026. The CfP calls it the premier academic forum for research that examines information systems through the lens of economics, and what a paper becomes here is small: one extended abstract of at most six double-spaced pages, submitted for presentation and discussion, with no proceedings and no copyright transfer. So the arrival cost is not a vocabulary switch but a compression: the whole argument, its design and its magnitudes, in roughly the length of one journal introduction.

**How this page differs from every sibling in the bank**: the other QBv pages rest on exemplar packs with measured budgets, and this one rests on a three-page CfP alone. Every length and rule below is therefore a DESK RULE, published and binding, except the two estimates marked as this page's own. Nothing here is a pack observation, because there is no pack.

**What is missing**: no accepted WISE extended abstract is stored anywhere this page can cite, so the internal shape of a competitive submission (section headers, figure count, reference count) is unrecorded.

## Content

### 1 · What this desk buys and what it costs

**Presentation without publication**: the submission spends no journal novelty.

```text
  ✅ BUYS     a room of IS-economics discussants before the journal rounds
  💸 COSTS    six pages of compression · the exclusivity attestations below
  🔓 KEEPS    all rights · no proceedings, so the journal path stays clean
```

Because nothing is published, the strategic question a submitting author answers is not "is this ready?" but "is the ICIS/WITS slate clear, and would arguing about this work improve it?".

### 2 · The gates are attestations, not reviews

**Three eligibility facts only the authors can state**: the desk asks, it does not check.

```text
  ① this work is not submitted or scheduled at ICIS 2026 or WITS 2026
  ② this work holds no revision invitation, conditional accept, or accept
  ③ this submission keeps each author within ~two co-authored works
```

A consuming narrative page records who attested each gate and when, because the desk's form will ask again at the portal.

### 3 · Where an agentic-AI field experiment enters

**Theme ① by name**: the CfP's first thematic area names agentic systems explicitly, and two other areas overlap.

```text
  primary    ① AI, Automation, and Economic Transformation
             ── "agentic systems" and "algorithmic decision-making" verbatim
  adjacent   ③ Data, Analytics, and Digital Experimentation
             ── "digital experiments in shaping managerial decisions"
  adjacent   ⑥ Technology, Society, and Sustainability ── "healthcare IT"
```

The theme choice belongs to the consuming narrative page, not here; this division only records which doors exist.
