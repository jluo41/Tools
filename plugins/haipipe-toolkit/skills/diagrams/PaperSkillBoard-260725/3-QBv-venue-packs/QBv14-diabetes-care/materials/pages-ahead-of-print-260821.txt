Online Ahead of Print

ADA publishes articles online ahead of print in order to make new research availble more quickly than printing allows. Diabetes articles containing original research are published online within 3–5 days after peer review acceptance. Diabetes Online Ahead of Print articles are the authors' original accepted manuscript and have not yet been copyedited or typeset. Diabetes Care articles containing original research are published online within 5–6 weeks from peer review acceptance. Diabetes Care Online Ahead of Print articles have been copyedited, typeset, and proofread by the authors. Clinical Diabetes and Diabetes Spectrum began publishing articles online ahead of print in December 2016.

Online Ahead of Print articles are citable by unique DOIs (digital object identifiers). DOIs for ADA journal articles begin with 10.2337, followed by the article number assigned when the manuscript was originally submitted online via the manuscript submission system (e.g., 10.2337/dc16-1234).
