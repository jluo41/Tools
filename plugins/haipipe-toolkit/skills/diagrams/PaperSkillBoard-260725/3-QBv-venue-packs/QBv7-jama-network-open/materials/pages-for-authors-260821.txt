About JAMA Network Open

Eli N. Perencevich, MD, MS

Editor in Chief

Video Interview

Stephan D. Fihn, MD, MPH

Executive Deputy Editor

JAMA Network Open is an international, peer-reviewed, open access, general medical journal that publishes research on clinical care, innovation in health care, health policy, and global health across all health disciplines and countries for clinicians, investigators, and policy makers. JAMA Network Open is a member of the JAMA Network, a consortium of peer-reviewed, general medical and specialty publications.

Journal Frequency and Access.
JAMA Network Open is published online every weekday. All articles are published under an open access license and available immediately at publication. All research articles that are accepted for publication require payment of an article processing charge (APC). Waivers and discounts on open access article processing charges are available for eligible authors from Hinari Core Offer countries . Additional information about the journal's open access options and APCs is available here .

Editorial Information. JAMA Network Open publishes original research articles and invited commentary on more than 40 medical and health subject areas. See complete list below. JAMA Network Open 's Journal Impact Factor is 11.7, highest among large volume general medicine open access journals. Information on the types of articles published and editorial policies is available in the journal’s Instructions for Authors . JAMA Network Open follows the same peer review, editorial, and publishing standards of JAMA and the JAMA Network journals.

Editorial Team. The Editor in Chief of JAMA Network Open is Eli N. Perencevich, MD, MS. Dr Perencevich is Director, Center for Access and Delivery Research & Evaluation (CADRE) at the Iowa City VA Health Care System, and Associate Chair for Clinical and Health Services Research, Professor of Internal Medicine-General Internal Medicine, Department of Internal Medicine, and Director, Program in Health Services and Outcomes Research at University of Iowa Carver College of Medicine. The Executive Deputy Editor of JAMA Network Open is Stephan D. Fihn, MD, MPH. Dr Fihn is a practicing general internist who is Professor in the departments of Medicine and of Health Services at the University of Washington. For a complete listing of the journal’s Editors, Statistical Reviewers, and Editorial Board, see Editors and Publishers .

Mission Statement.
The mission of JAMA Network Open is to improve health, health care, and health equity worldwide through open access dissemination of high quality, innovative, general medical research and commentary by and for a broad range of clinicians, researchers, policy makers, and public health and health care leaders.

Guidance and Benefits for Peer Reviewers

Why Publish in JAMA Network Open ?

Building on a tradition of editorial excellence, JAMA Network Open provides authors the impact and reach of JAMA and the JAMA Network specialty journals as well as prompt decisions, rapid publication, author-focused services, and high-quality peer review and editing.

Impact and Reach

Journal Impact Factor of 11.7, highest among large volume general medicine open access journals

Broad reach through related commentary, podcasts, email alerts, news releases, posts on Apple News, and more than 69,000 social media followers (Facebook, X, LinkedIn)

More than 41 million annual article views and downloads

Open access for all articles

Indexed and covered in Altmetric, CLOCKSS, Directory of Open Access Journals, EBSCO, EurekAlert, Google Scholar, Ovid, PubMed/MEDLINE, PubMed Central, Scopus, Sherpa Romeo, and Science Citation Index Expanded

Top Altmetric scores among open access general medical journals— JAMA Network Open published 38 of the top 50 articles among open access general medical journals

Number of citations, Altmetric score, page views, PDF downloads, and online comments tracked for your published article

Extensive press coverage: In 2025 and 2026, JAMA Network Open received more than 44,000 mentions in over 4,800 outlets consisting of news media, broadcast TV and radio stations, blogs, news aggregators, and more

Invited Commentaries published to augment and direct attention to research articles

Online Commenting available to continue review and discussion post-publication

International authors: 69% of submissions come from outside the US

Prompt Decisions and Rapid Publication Timelines

Highly selective peer review and editorial evaluation, with 12% acceptance rate for all submissions

Median time to first decision: 2 days without external peer review; 46 days with review

Median time from submission to acceptance: 90 days; 59 days from acceptance to publication

Expedited peer review and publication for select clinical trials, other major studies, or reports with urgent public health importance

Coordination with meeting presentation: Opportunity to publish new research simultaneously with your presentation

Author Service

JAMA Network Open offers unparalleled reach and an author-friendly approach, from manuscript submission through publication.

User-friendly manuscript submission system and process

Prompt, expert review and consideration from our experienced editors

Highest standards followed to improve your accepted manuscript's accuracy, reliability, and readability

CME credit available for lead authors who publish in JAMA Network Open

High-quality peer review, manuscript editing, and graphics reproduction

Open access: Authors of research articles pay an article processing charge for immediate open access on the day of publication (waivers and discounts are available for authors from resource-poor countries)

Display of content on fast, device-responsive website

Citations, views, and Altmetric score displayed with every article

Deposit to PubMed Central: The journal deposits all published research articles into PubMed Central (PMC) for authors

Online extras: Ability to post supplemental content with articles and download article tables and figures as PowerPoint slides

The JAMA Network advantage: Authors of papers not accepted by JAMA or another JAMA Network Journal may request to have their papers automatically referred to JAMA Network Open for consideration

Dedicated media team to promote your article to the news media and on social media

Commitment to Equity

JAMA Network journals welcome manuscript submissions on equity, diversity, and inclusion (EDI).

For more information, see the JAMA Network EDI Editors, Policies, and Topic Collection
