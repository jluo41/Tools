# The workbench storage roster · one row per lane or category name

The single list of Page material names (design.excalidraw, JL 260815).
A subfolder of a Page's home folder is board material only if its name is here.
A row identifies storage, not necessarily a separate Workbench Skill: a category
Workbench may directly own one of its internal lanes.
`kind`: PRIMARY = originals a person makes, committed; DERIVED = projections of the page's text, regenerable, gitignorable.

`page.toml` is optional Page registration, not a workbench directory. A declared
`source-content:` identifies an editable file import under materials; this is
distinct from an immutable evidence capture and is not automatically evidence.
`delivery/web/` is the Page-owned static reading export, built by
`haipipe-page/cli/page.py build`; it does not add another public workbench.

**📃 Page is one Workbench over context, plan, and evidence materials:** Citation/Bib,
Value, and Display are internal lanes of its Evidence Space. The typed Evidence Item table
(`draft/<stem>-evidence-items.md`) is where SHAPE specifies the ready target
and SURVEY plans Supporting Runs, one Local Input, and one Local Run.

The generic public Page Workbench skills are exactly two, and each pairs with
one served workbench: `workbench-page` (📃 Page with its three Spaces and
Runs panels, 📤 Delivery, 📂 Folder: everything `servers/workbench/task-page` renders) and
`workbench-studio` (🎨 Studio: `servers/workbench`). The All-runs view,
Delivery, Folder, Chat, Draw, LaTeX, Word, Slide, Render, Skill,
Citation/Bib, Value, and Display are internal references owned by those two
(`workbench-page/ref/run-space.md`, `ref/delivery.md`, `ref/folder.md`).
Design (`skills/2_theme/design/workbench-design`), Insight
(`skills/2_theme/insight/workbench-insight`), Paper
(`skills/2_theme/paper/workbench-paper`) and Labeling (`haipipe-labeling`'s
`workbench-labeling`) own their workbench skills beside their writers;
each pairs with `servers/workbench-<x>`. Meeting is project/SPACE material owned by
`haipipe-project-meeting`.

Category and meta Workbenches need not correspond one-to-one with storage rows.
`workbench-page/ref/folder.md` is the 📂 meta-surface over this list;
📃 Page owns Context, Shape, Citation/Bib, Value, Display, run lineage, and
the embedded Skills record; `draft/skill/` retains its own storage and
writer contract without a top-level picker row;
Delivery (`workbench-page/ref/delivery.md`) owns latex, word, slide,
and render; Studio owns Draw and Chat; and the Runs panels and the All-runs view
(`workbench-page/ref/run-space.md`) join Tickets to Results. Design joins its existing intent, candidate,
verification, Run, and delivery lanes in `workbench-design`; it is a
domain category extension rather than a sixth generic Page workbench. Each public
category owns its internal lane contracts, writers, and gates without minting
duplicate skills.
