# Ideation · Constitution

## A01 · A direction is a Block; each idea is a Job

> “I’m thinking about to make the idea to be one specific block them self. And one idea will be a job and then in the task, they are a series of skills to evaluate it and check it. Do you get what I mean? I hope each idea will be developed to one paper later. What is your thoughts”

Recorded 2026-11-24.

**Rule:** New durable portfolios use a direction Block, one Job per admitted
idea, and Tasks named for their research questions or deliverables. Skills are
methods applied within those Tasks. Assessment Runs follow the shared Run
contract; they do not duplicate Work or Discovery evidence.

**Paper boundary:** Keep the Idea Job when it becomes a paper. The person's
selection authorizes a linked Paper Block. Evaluation needs no pre-existing
Paper page. Defer, abandon, split and merge remain explicit outcomes with
preserved identities and history.

**Compatibility:** Existing multi-card Task portfolios remain readable. Never
move them, rewrite old decisions, or manufacture a Paper merely to validate
an idea. See [the contract](references/idea-block.md).

**Guard:** `scripts/check_idea_block.py`; `tests/test_idea_block.py` and
`tests/test_idea_job_selection.py`. A structural pass does not establish
scientific merit or demonstrate a dedicated Idea Board UI.
