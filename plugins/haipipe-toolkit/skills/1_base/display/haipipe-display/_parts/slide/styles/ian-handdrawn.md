# ian-handdrawn

Refined ink-and-pencil teaching-note page: small exact text, fine lines, light pastel labels, wide white space

## Design Aesthetic

A premium article or teaching-note illustration, not a filled slide. Fine black ink and pencil line art with delicate
hatching, stable but slightly irregular. Object-based diagrams (folders, boxes, drawers, cards, dials, trays,
documents) explain the idea; the central diagram is small and calm, about 50-60% of the page width and 35-45% of its
height, with large negative space around it.

## Background

- Color: Very Light Warm White (#FBFAF5); never yellow, beige or tea-stained paper
- Texture: extremely subtle grain; no full-page border or frame
- Only tiny notebook marks in the corners: a faint grey grid, dots, ruler ticks

## Typography

### Primary Font (Headlines)

Neat hard-pen handwriting, medium size, centred, with one short pale blue hand-drawn underline. Never a giant poster
headline, even when the title is short.

### Secondary Font (Body)

Small, readable hard-pen handwriting. A short subtitle under the title, often three terms joined by "·".

## Color Palette

| Role | Color | Hex | Usage |
|------|-------|-----|-------|
| Background | Warm White | #FBFAF5 | Paper |
| Line | Near Black | #222222 | Ink lines, text |
| Label 1 | Pale Blue | #CFE0F0 | Marker labels, the title underline |
| Label 2 | Sage Green | #D5E3CF | Marker labels |
| Label 3 | Peach | #F6D9C8 | Marker labels, the focal object |
| Label 4 | Lavender | #E3DAF0 | Marker labels, the closing line |
| Accent | Red Orange | #E8603C | A tiny star, dot or underline only |

## Visual Elements

- Fine hand-drawn object drawings with hatching, not flat icons
- Slim, quiet arrows and thin dashed connecting curves
- Small pastel marker labels holding one to three words
- Props (cards, books, screens) blank or with simple line marks; no fake writing
- No people, or one tiny figure (or only a hand) at an edge

## Density Guidelines

- Two to four structure groups, one main idea
- Title up to 8 words; labels one to three words; at most three small annotations
- English text breaks at small sizes: keep every word inside an object to one short word, or leave the object blank
  (tried 261029: "records" and "cases" on small boxes came out as "scop" and "ase")

## Style Rules

### Do

- Keep the same shell on every page: paper, title, underline, corner marks, margins
- Vary only the central diagram by what the slide says
- Leave large white space

### Don't

- Use saturated colour blocks, shadows, gradients or neon
- Enlarge a short title into a cover headline
- Crowd the page with bullets or many characters
- Add slide numbers, footers or logos (this folder's base rule, unlike the source)

## Best For

Calm explanations a reader studies: Report illustrations, article figures, settled concepts. Too quiet for a Studio
slide that must pull a reader in within seconds.
