# haipipe-plugin-design · version history

Recovered from the SKILL.md frontmatter summary on 260827, when the family retired the `summary:` field: version history lives here and is never loaded at invocation.

- 0.10.0 (JL 260828, "要不然谁能知道，对吧？谁能看得出来呀？"): every pool entry gains a `from:` line beside `trying:` — provenance as an honesty label, never a warrant: `insight · <row id>` / `knowledge · <mechanism>` / `intuition`, composable with `×`, and intuition ALWAYS carries its information regime (`brief-only` | `informed`) because the two intuitions are different things (JL: "啥都没有的时候你有一种 intuition，看了一部分东西之后再有 intuition，效果可能完全不一样"). Derivable from converge's finalist-to-ancestor map + the unit's regime; judge's pool checks go five → six (a `from: insight` must name a row the grant reaches); pre-260828 pools backfill from their own ideation records as maintenance, not re-judged. The DU16 (brief-only) vs DU17/18 (informed) pair on B00 is the first natural experiment for the research question the line opens.
- 0.9.0 (JL 260828, "我们这个 plug-in direction 是不是也要删掉了呀"): absorbed haipipe-plugin-direction whole and that plugin was DELETED, git the only copy (the retired-page-type precedent). One folder, one law: the card grammar, release gate and five card laws now live here as §card; the three stale spots the old file carried into 0.7.0 (a direction/ storage tree, DR<NN> numbering, "check.py reads direction/DR*.md") were corrected in transit. The card laws are unchanged in substance and keep their numbers as "card law 1-5"; the unit laws are now "unit law 1-3". haipipe-plugin-direction's own version history (0.1.0-0.7.0) lives in git at its deletion commit.
- 0.8.0 (JL 260828, the one-thread-one-folder merge): card.md joins the unit anatomy as the folder's first file; README loses `direction:`; the designer's only card write is the released→landed state flip; checker rules unit-no-direction/-ghost retired for unit-no-card + unit-realized-before-release. See haipipe-plugin-direction 0.7.0 for the card side.
- 0.7.1 (260828, checker-compatibility repair): the pool unit KEEPS spec.md (acceptance = the five pool checks, still compiled-never-invented) and a DISCLAIMING evidence.md on the ignore-card precedent — check.py's unit-file-missing requires both files, and a law the checker fails on round one is a defect in the law.
- 0.7.0 (JL 260828, "我不要求你测，我现在只要求你 brainstorm new message"): the POOL unit — what a `brainstorm` card lands. The lightest unit in the plugin, and every absence is deliberate: no evidence.md (inspiration is not warrant, nothing in a pool is a claim), no prospect.md (a forecast is per-bet), no control cell or allocation or power arithmetic, and ZERO reuse. The judge checks only count, newness against the whole fielded set, MUTUAL distinctness, the trying: line, and the rails. Which entries get fielded and against what control is the downstream FIELDING decision, and pulling it back into the pool is the failure this unit prevents.
- 0.6.1 (260828, same day, after the divergence sweep landed 15 verified sources as QD5): three literature-driven refinements — goal-first becomes a REQUIRED angle (the goal-setting reinterpretation finds the active ingredient may be a specific goal, not divergence as such), five is a FLOOR with no ceiling (diminishing returns, and a cap would be a second judgment on generation), and converge KEEPS MORE THAN ONE finalist unless the rails forbid it (generators are poor at picking their own best; the field picks between variants, not the arm). §ideation now points at QD5.
- 0.6.0 (JL 260828, "design 不是 conclusion——它有一个收和开的过程"): `ideation.md` joins the generate unit's anatomy — the lane's first lawful DIVERGENCE surface. Two movements: DIVERGE (five-plus candidates, two-plus angles, deferred judgment, intuition labeled, grant-only does not reach a sketchbook, fixation guard: no re-reading fielded copy) then CONVERGE (every candidate dispositioned, finalists mapped to warrant legs, rails only now, finalists become the variant set, discards stay). Both information regimes design: brief-only warrant-insight (direction 0.5.0) seeds diverge from the Brief's goal and emits the missing insight in the same round. `unit-generate-no-ideation` checker rule OWED, prove-FAIL-first.
- 0.5.0 (JL 260828, after rounds 2-3 both retreated to fielded copy): generate units owe a spec `novelty:` block (surface · mechanism · the card's two warrant anchors); byte-identity to any fielded template FAILS — the poverty exit is EMIT, never quotation; composes with 0.4.1's variant sets. Spec section gains the GD3 ruling of the same day: the judge audits the acceptance list against its declared sources — an invented item that narrows is a finding, one that widens fails. `unit-generate-no-novelty` checker rule OWED, prove-FAIL-first.
- 0.4.0 (260827, cold-read repair): the ex-post verdict got its on-disk home — the README `judged:` line, written by the JUDGE (fresh context, never the arm) in the same edit that flips `state: judged`; and the forecast's score got its home — a dated `scored:` line appended to prospect.md at read-back, the same post-acceptance bookkeeping class as staleness clearing.
- 0.3.0 (260827): `prospect.md` joins the unit anatomy — the ex-ante forecast (walkthrough, mechanism, predicted effect + uncertainty, failure conditions) required at haipipe-design-workflow's GD4 before acceptance, with three guardrails (grant-only citation, forecast-typed output that never lands on an InsightBoard, scored-not-cited). Not retroactive: pre-260827 acceptances and record-mode units are exempt. A `unit-no-prospect` checker rule is owed and must first be proven to fail.
- 0.2.0 (JL 260824): eight checker rules over design/, including unit-dead-reference, written after a unit cited its own card one level short and left the wager unreachable. 0.1.0: the artifact plugin, display-shaped plus a spec.
