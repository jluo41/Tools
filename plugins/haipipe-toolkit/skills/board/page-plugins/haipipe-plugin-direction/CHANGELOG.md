# haipipe-plugin-direction · version history

Recovered on 260827 when the family retired the `summary:` field: version history lives here and is never loaded at invocation.

- 0.6.0 (JL 260828, "我们是完全摒弃任何现在的 message"): law 5, the `brainstorm` stance — the lane was charging EXPERIMENT-design duties (control cell, comparator, predicted effect) to MESSAGE-design work, which kept pulling fielded copy back in until nothing new got written. A brainstorm card commissions a POOL: `pool-target:` new messages, `inspiration:` replacing `grant:` (insights say who and what is known, licensing and forbidding nothing about wording), `avoid:` naming the fielded set as a NEGATIVE list. Three rules: zero reuse, no comparator, inspiration-not-warrant.
- 0.5.0 (JL 260828, same day): law 4 gains the POOR information regime — `warrant-insight: brief-only` names the Brief's audience/goal row when no insight row exists (board-local, always legal in a grant), and the same round emits the missing insight as a BR00 need: designing without information and registering what you lacked are one act. Warrant-theory, novelty, ideation, rails never relax.
- 0.4.0 (JL 260828, after field-test rounds 2-3 both retreated to quoting fielded copy): the `generate` stance — law 4: warrant = one named insight row + one Discovery QA theory file, both inside the grant; `expected effect:` direction admissible only `theory-typed`; a card that cannot produce both legs is an explore/bet-against wearing the wrong word. Law 1 gains release-binds-existing-cards (the GD1 inversion friction of the same day). Checker rules `card-generate-no-theory` / `card-generate-no-insight` OWED, prove-FAIL-first.
- 0.3.0 (260827, cold-read repair): the recorded BLANKET release legalized — a person names a set of cards in writing, the run transcribes `released: <person> (blanket, <YYMMDD>)` onto each as a clerical record, the person's words cited in the DS page's Log; a machine's inference is never a release. Law 1 and the Writer section carry it.
- 0.2.0 (JL 260824): eight checker rules over direction/, each proven to FAIL on a one-way-broken board; record mode for pre-contract artifacts.
- 0.1.0: the card grammar, the release gate, the grant chain.
