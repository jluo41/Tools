# The plugin roster · one row per name

The single list of page-plugin names (design.excalidraw, JL 260815).
A subfolder of a page's home folder is board material only if its name is here.
`kind`: PRIMARY = originals a person makes, committed; DERIVED = projections of the page's text, regenerable, gitignorable.

**Probe is a logical family over two existing plugin rows, not a third folder:**
`pagex/` is its accepted-Page lane in OUTLINE; `probe/` is its Task/Discovery
QA lane in PROBE/EVIDENCE. Their storage stays separate so an accepted Page
binding can never masquerade as a bank answer.

Writers for the DERIVED document trio live in `skills/board/page-plugins/_shared-export/` (`md2tex.py`, `md2docx.py`, `docx2pdf.py`); the board calls them by path.
`--paper-root` is discovered by walking up from the page for a `0-*.bib`; a page outside any paper exports cite-less rather than refusing.
Per-plugin SKILLs ship under `page-plugins/` (JL 260815): draw · slide · chat · latex · word · bibex · probe · outline · skill · display · pagex · task · meeting · direction · design · render · evidence, each `haipipe-plugin-<name>` and delta-only over this contract; the roster stays the single list of NAMES, a row going 🟢 brings its skill with it, and logging/_fixture gain theirs when their rows go live.
SIX skills have no row on purpose, and for the same reason — they own no subfolder. They are the board's PRESENTER plugins, the second of the two plugin kinds (haipipe-plugin §🔌): a LANE plugin owns one rostered folder's law (storage, writer, gate); a PRESENTER owns one surface over a CATEGORY of lanes and stores nothing. `haipipe-plugin-folder` (JL 260816) is the 📂 meta-surface OVER this list; `haipipe-plugin-value` (JL 260819) is the 🧮 surface over the `## Values` blocks that already live inside probe cards (since 260831 a segment inside 🧾 Evidence); `haipipe-plugin-evidence` (JL 260831) is the ONE 🧾 tab presenting the bibex, probe, value, display and pagex lanes as segments; `haipipe-plugin-delivery` (JL 260831) is the ONE 📤 tab presenting latex, word, slide and render the same way (its 🎞 segment carries the deck's ✨ authoring bar); `haipipe-plugin-studio` (JL 260831 evening: "put both of them into the studio, as one page") is the ONE 🎨 tab staging the drawing above the chat, both live at once; and `haipipe-plugin-code` (260831, tab pending) is the ⚙️ surface owed to the LOWER code part (scripts/ · runs/ · results/), which ships when the first real page runs/ exists — a tab over folders no page holds yet would teach nothing. Presentation only, every writer and gate stays with its lane. A number filed a second time is one thing filed twice; what was missing was never a folder but one more level of ADDRESS, `PP<NN>.v<n>`.
