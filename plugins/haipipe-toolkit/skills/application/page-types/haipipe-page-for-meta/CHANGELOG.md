## 0.2.0 — 2026-08-21

- The Insight Roster is gone. JL split the question register out of this page
  and by rung: `MT01-question-data` through `MT04-question-wisdom`
  (`haipipe-page-for-question`), each facing the ladder group that answers it.
- Meta is inventory only, which its own "raises no question" rule always
  demanded; the fixed outline ends at Known Limits.
- The board rollup that the Roster provided lives on the wisdom register's
  Queue division, because W is where every chain terminates.
- Group token declared: `MT`, shared with the four registers in `0-MT-meta/`.

- New Page Type: the one Meta Page heading an InsightBoard.
- Created by splitting `haipipe-page-for-brief`: its divisions 6-7 (Insight
  Need Map, Core PageX Inputs) served the insight side while divisions 1-5
  served delivery. JL split the Application into an InsightBoard and a
  DesignBoard, and the Brief split on its own seam.

  required section on every Board Page.

  concludes, and raises no question of its own.
