REPLY BOXES — the shape every chat reply to JL opens with
=========================================================
Ruled 260818, in two corrections one after the other:
  "I want the style like this, highlight your answer, highlight
   what I need to do."
  "I think you should also highlight your quick answer."
So: TWO boxes, not one. A bold sentence on line 1 is not enough,
because bold still disappears in a scroll.


--S The shape, in order ---------------------------------------------

RULES ONLY: a line above and a line below. NO left or right edge.
Ruled 260819: "你这个 box 能不能只保留上下，不要左右，然后把它变得越宽越好？"
Side pipes force every line to be padded to one width, which caps the
text at about 58 columns and wraps sentences that would otherwise fit.

══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
✅ ANSWER · <the answer to what was asked, one line>
<at most 2 more lines: the number, the path, the verdict>
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
🧑 YOURS · <how long, or the word "nothing">
<the literal thing to type, click, or decide>
<what happens if it is skipped>
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

── 🤖 MINE · <n> items, no input needed ─────────────────────────────────────
  1️⃣ <what the assistant does>        <how long>
  2️⃣ ...

<then, and only then, the state / findings / evidence diagrams,
 still inside the ≤3 diagram budget>


--S The five rules ---------------------------------------------------

1. Never omit the YOURS box. With nothing to do it still appears and
   says `nothing`, so it is never scanned for.
2. YOURS holds the LITERAL thing: the exact command, the exact line,
   the exact path. Never a description of it.
3. MINE is a numbered list with time estimates. It is what makes
   `nothing` in the YOURS box credible instead of evasive.
4. Both rules are 78 columns. Two widths read as two unrelated things.
5. No padding inside. Text starts at column 1, under the rule.

--S When to skip both boxes ------------------------------------------

- a one-word factual answer
- a pure "sketch this" request, where the diagram IS the answer
- mid-work progress notes
