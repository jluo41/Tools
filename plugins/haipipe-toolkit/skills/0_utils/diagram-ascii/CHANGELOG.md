## [0.2.0] — 2026-08-18

- New `## Reply format — open with TWO boxes`, placed BEFORE `When to Use` so it
  is read before anything else in the file. Ruled by JL on 260818 in two
  corrections, one after the other: "I want the style like this, highlight your
  answer, highlight what I need to do", then "I think you should also highlight
  your quick answer." A bold sentence on line 1 was not enough, because bold
  still disappears in a scroll.
- New `ref/09-reply-boxes.txt`: the shape, a worked example, the four rules that
  make it work, and when NOT to use it.
- The four rules are the part that will drift, so they are stated in both files:
  never omit the YOURS box (with nothing to do it says `nothing`); YOURS holds
  the LITERAL command and never a description of it; MINE carries time estimates,
  which is what makes `nothing` credible instead of evasive; and both boxes are
  62 columns wide, because two widths read as two unrelated things.
- The skill's `description` now names the reply shape, so the skill is reachable
  when a reply needs it and not only when a diagram does.
