By problematization
===================

One framing method card. The Paper workbench shows it in the shared Guide › Method,
under Framing methods; its papers are the rows of `../../../related/papers.md` whose
`group` is `by problematization`. A claim names its source in brackets; "(ours)" marks the
workbench's own judgment.

family: Framing: Which question is worth asking?
move: Before taking a gap in earlier work as the question, name the assumption under
  that work and ask whether challenging it gives a better question.
comes from: Sandberg & Alvesson 2011, gap-spotting or problematization; Alvesson &
  Sandberg 2011, generating research questions
reads: the candidate ideas · the literature they build on
returns: the admitted question and the assumption it challenges or the gap it fills
test now: T0 asked
test in use: T5 answered


What the literature says
------------------------

rationale: Most research questions are constructed by spotting gaps in earlier work,
  which extends that work without questioning it [Sandberg 2011]; problematization
  identifies and challenges the assumptions under existing theory to generate questions
  that can lead to more interesting theory [Alvesson 2011].
context: Management and organization studies, where the authors read how published
  papers construct their questions [Sandberg 2011; Alvesson 2011].
steps: 1. Name the literature the idea builds on. 2. Write the gap it fills. 3. Write
  the assumption that literature makes. 4. Ask whether the question is better as a
  challenge to that assumption. 5. Admit one question.
strengths: It makes the kind of contribution explicit before any work is planned, so a
  gap-filling paper is chosen, not fallen into (ours).
limitations: Challenging an assumption is harder to evidence than filling a gap, and a
  field may judge the result by gap-filling norms (ours).


Applied to AI
-------------

agent: An ideation agent generates and tests candidate ideas; a different agent reviews
  them; the person admits one.
steps: 1. Generate ideas. 2. For each, name its gap and its assumption. 3. Test novelty.
  4. Review. 5. The person signs the admitted idea (G0).
returns: the Ideation Space's ideas and the admitted one.
verify: The reviewer did not generate the idea; the person signs G0.
risk: A model trained on published papers may reproduce the gap-spotting habit of that
  literature (ours).
evidence on ai: No study tests problematization done by an agent (ours).
