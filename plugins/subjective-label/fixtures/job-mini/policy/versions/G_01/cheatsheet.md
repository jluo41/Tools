# G_01 cheatsheet · empathy

🟢 HIGH   a DESCRIBED ACT of noticing or responding to the patient's emotional state (asked, paused, waited, stayed)
🔵 LOW    warmth asserted without a described act ('kind', 'caring' alone)
⚪ NONE    no emotional content about the physician; logistics, billing, skill-only praise
🗺 regions H=act described · L=warmth asserted only · N=no emotional content · HL=act implied not described
