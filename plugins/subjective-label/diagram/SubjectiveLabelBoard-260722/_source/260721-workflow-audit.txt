─§ ⑥ THE INVARIANT — and what we deliberately did not do ─────────────────────

   S6 scoring     items:  anchor 24 ⊂ gallery 48         ⚠️ own upstream
                  labels: the same person who wrote the rule

   S8 external    the ceiling is chosen after the fact, from datasets that do
                  not measure the construct

  ❌ THE ANTI-PATTERN — "the number went up, so it is working"
     v01 0.874 → v02 0.811 → v03 0.874 → v04 0.937    📈 looks like proof
     all four measured on the SAME 24 items the guideline was written from.
     Under those conditions an upward curve is the EXPECTED result of editing.

  THE CEILING TEST — for S8
   ① CONSTRUCT   the dataset labels the thing we label, not a cousin of it.
   ② HEADROOM    below ~0.4 the humans did not agree either.
   go_emotions           ① ❌ emotion ≠ a Big-Five trait      ② ✅ 0.46
   popquorn_politeness   ① ❌ politeness ≠ agreeableness      ② ❌ 0.2433

  💀 DEAD — do not resurrect
  · 💀 GoEmotions as an agreeableness / openness proxy. Tried twice (0.30, 0.25).
       B03 marks it `construct_valid: false`.
  · 💀 Declaring CONVERGED by clearing a human ceiling below 0.4.
  · 💀 Re-randomising the anchor set every round — a moving anchor makes
       versions incomparable, which is worse than the leak.

  🟡 LOGGED, NOT ADOPTED
  · 🟡 Model-specific guideline versions — "V1.1 for haiku, V1.2 for GPT-4.5"
       (JL, 07-17 [134:32]). A guideline needing a per-model rewrite is a
