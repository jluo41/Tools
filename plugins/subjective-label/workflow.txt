🏗🔍 subjective-label plugin · v0.4.0
=====================================

One umbrella · two sibling doors · six journey phases · one signed crossing.


FAMILY
──────

  /subjective-label
          │
          ├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
          ▼                                                          ▼
  🏗 LABEL BUILDING                                          🔍 LABEL SCANNING
  "Is this what the human means?"                          "Was it executed reliably?"

  P0 CONTRACT                                               P3 TEST
      │                                                         │  GOLD · SCORE
      ▼                                                         ▼
  P1 ROUND × N                                              P4 SCAN
      │  PREPARE · JUDGE · LEARN · CLOSE                       │  risk queue
      │  another round ───────────────────↺                     ▼
      ▼                                                     P5 AUDIT

      │                                                         ▼
  SIGNED LABEL HANDOFF ─────────────────────────────────────────▶ D*
     G* · D_cal* · corpus · schema · sealed-test checksum


WORD LAW
────────

  journey phase   Contract · Round · Freeze · Test · Scan · Audit
                  each names its own authority artifact

  round step      PREPARE · JUDGE · LEARN · CLOSE
                  resumable actions inside one Round

  test step       GOLD (human, blind) · SCORE (executors, blind)
                  ordered actions inside Test

  gate            G0-G6, checkable transitions between artifacts

  route           another round · HOLD · repair · rescan · reopen · complete

  item event      one human-first or final judgment

Pick, seal, label, rules, measure, and next are not journey phases.


THE CROSSING
────────────

  Building writes:  handoff/label-v1.yaml
  Scanning reads:   its exact checksum

  carries: corpus + schema + G* + D_cal* + sealed-test manifest checksum
           stopping evidence + lineage + human freeze signature

  never carries: protected test ids/text · drafts · model-majority gold


USER SURFACE
────────────

  /subjective-label              auto-route one next runnable action
  /label-building                contract, calibrate, freeze
  /label-scanning                test, scan, audit
  /subjective-label-workflow     inspect phases and gates


STOP RULE
─────────

Stop at a human gate, implementation HOLD, invalidation, bounded action close,
or audited D*. Never convert silence, consensus, elapsed time, or a round limit
into semantic authority or a reliability claim.
