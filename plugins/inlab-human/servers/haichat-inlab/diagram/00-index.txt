The In-Lab Console = a clinician-facing window over the haipipe pipeline,
embeddable as a per-thread iframe beside a HAI-Chat conversation. This folder
explains WHAT it is, HOW it came to be, and HOW it's designed — one topic per
file, scan top-to-bottom.

─§ The one-paragraph version ─────────────────────────────────────

  Pick a HUMAN (patient / physician) → read their RECORD → see the CASES cut
  from it → attach a LABEL (subjective-label panel) or a SCORE (model endpoint).
  Nothing here computes a score; the endpoint does. Nothing here is baked in;
  every store is mounted by env. The buttons and the chat agent reach the
  endpoint through the SAME engine, or the demo is a lie.
