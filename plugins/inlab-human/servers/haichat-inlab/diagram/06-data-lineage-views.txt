🧬  Data lineage in the UI — one dataset, four stages
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The four DATA views are the SAME dataset shown at successive pipeline
stages. Put Source next to Record (drag the tab) and you watch the cleaning
happen row-for-row. Each view has a banner naming its store.

─§ view ↔ store ↔ transform ──────────────────────────────────────

  +-----------+------------------+--------------------------------------+
  | 👁️ View    | 🗂️ Store          | ⚙️ What you see                       |
  +-----------+------------------+--------------------------------------+
  | 📄 Raw     | 0-RawDataStore   | files exactly as they arrived        |
  | 🗂️ Source  | 1-SourceStore    | ProcName_to_ProcDf — parsed tables   |
  | 🧾 Record  | 2-RecStore       | RecordFn: cleaned, PID-keyed, binned |
  | 📌 Case    | 3-CaseStore      | one case = one annotation/pred point |
  +-----------+------------------+--------------------------------------+

    📄 ──▶ 🗂️ ──▶ 🧾 ──▶ 📌
   arrive  parse  clean  cut
   (⚙️ SourceFn · HumanFn+RecordFn · TriggerFn+CaseFn between the arrows)

─§ ⚠️ The as-of discipline (Raw / Source view) ───────────────────

  the chart is rendered AS OF the prediction index date. In the raw view,
  rows that POSTDATE it are FLAGGED, not hidden:

    row_date ≤ index_date   →  ✅ the model saw this
    row_date >  index_date   →  ⚠️ _after_index  (the future — model never saw it)

─§ The human-type axis (why patient AND physician fit) ───────────

  the pipeline is generic over a HUMAN. patient / physician are siblings,
  not different architectures — the choice is CONFIG, not schema:

        🧑 Human  (record layer is keyed by HumanID)
       ┌────────────┴────────────┐
    HmACIPtt (patient)        HmPhy (physician)
    RawHumanID = PatientID    RawHumanID = NPI
    streams: Dialogue/Note    streams: Review/Profile

  ⚠️ Records do NOT choose which humans. A Review stream no more "knows"
     it's a physician than CGM "knows" it's a patient. The human TYPE is
     picked by the mounted dataset; WHICH individuals, by the cohort/case
     config — never by the record schema.
