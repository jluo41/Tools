state: 🔴 OPEN · bounded draft; no Page release or Commission release

## Opening

Library pickup communications help members find their held books and help staff prepare the handoff.
This draft bounds eight proposed designs using explicit requirements and visible placeholders.
It does not establish which message works best or authorize any real delivery.

## Content

### 1 · Opportunity

The scenario asks for a small pickup communication portfolio for a community library.
The mandate authorizes this local planning exercise only.
No rate of missed pickups, member preference, or improvement estimate has been supplied.

### 2 · Audience Set and Behavior

Members need to locate pickup instructions and a deadline.
App users need a clear route to their pickup details.
Pickup desk staff need to prepare and confirm a handoff.
Library managers need to inspect the proposed scope and its evidence limits.
These audience roles are planning assumptions, not measured population findings.

### 3 · Outcome and Kill Criteria

The design outcome is eight inspectable proposals, each with one audience, one job, one primary venue, and one acceptance condition.
Stop or narrow a draft if it requires an unavailable operational fact, claims improved pickup effectiveness, or implies a live system exists.
Use named placeholders for branch, deadline, details URL, app destination, and sample counts until the library supplies them.
A future operational rollout is outside this draft's scope.

### 4 · Venue Scope

The eight venues are SMS, email, push notification, reminder, UI card, checklist, report, and dashboard.
The report concerns proposed scope and evidence gaps; it contains no invented study results.
The dashboard is a specification with clearly synthetic data and no live connection.
The reminder line initially asks for one draft; a rotating set of three to five variants is a venue recommendation to resolve before any real Commission.
Report and email length defaults are not asserted met by the short fixture excerpts.

### 5 · Promise

Each proposed item uses basis brief-only, stance generate, and mode compose.
The one acceptance condition per item is in the matching Design Item register.
Requirements are explicit drafting constraints, not empirical evidence.
The exercise ends with an uncommissioned proposal and a synthetic contract walkthrough.
No human release, sending, live Board change, deployment, or measurement is authorized.
