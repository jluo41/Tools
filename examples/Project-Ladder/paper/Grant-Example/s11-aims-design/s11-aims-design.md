## Aims and failure criteria

| Aim | Comparison | What would challenge it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Calibration under shift | Fixed predictor with and without recalibration | Improved calibration only on the fitting sample |
| Missing-input robustness | Baseline and masking-trained model | Improvement disappears under the declared deployment mechanism |

Both aims share the [evidence plan](/_board/workbench?path=examples%2FProject-Ladder%2Fpaper%2FGrant-Example&layout=focus&section=jobs&group=j11). Their feasibility must be assessed before scientific claims enter the application.
