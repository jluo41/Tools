## Comparison design

Freeze the baseline, data split, shifts and metrics before evaluating interventions. The calibration sample is separate from the test sample. Subgroup boundaries are prespecified.

## Alternatives

If usable shifted data are unavailable, narrow the proposal to a controlled protocol demonstration. If subgroup information is too sparse, report that limitation instead of asserting a difference.
