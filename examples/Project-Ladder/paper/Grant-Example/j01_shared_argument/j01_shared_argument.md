## Topic

The proposed project asks when a model's apparent robustness survives a change in population or missing-data pattern.

**Gap to verify:** whether current evaluations adequately separate calibration, missingness and subgroup uncertainty.

**Proposed contribution:** a reproducible evaluation protocol and a bounded pilot comparing interventions against fixed baselines.

**Argument:** deployment change → explicit failure modes → prespecified comparisons → interpretable limits.

The research question and contribution are content of this shared Story Job, not automatically separate Tasks.
