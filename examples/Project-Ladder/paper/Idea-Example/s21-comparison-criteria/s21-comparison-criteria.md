## Selection criteria

| Criterion | What the comparison needs |
| --- | --- |
| Contribution | The difference from verified closest work |
| Testability | A bounded contrast that could refute the claim |
| Feasibility | Inputs, resources and an informative minimum experiment |
| Scope | The populations and shifts covered by the evidence |

The portfolio can develop several candidates. Selection stays in each Idea Job's human decision record; this comparison creates no selection.
