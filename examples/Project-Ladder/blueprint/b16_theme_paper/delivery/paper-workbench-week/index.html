The whole workflow

Paper map

Two levels, like a country map. The highway map links the seven cities of the paper workflow; each road is a handoff, and the gates (G1 to G5) sit on the roads. Click a city to go into its metro: the lines and stations inside it.

Story Citythe paper BoardResearch Citywork and discoveryEvidence Cityvalue and cite JobsDisplay Cityfigures and tables, j01Draft Citythe version Job, one session per SectionDelivery Cityassemble, LaTeX, Word, submitRound Citythe co-author round

What the map shows

  • Draft City is the hub. Five roads meet there (Story, Evidence, Display, Round, and out to Delivery), which is why most problems sit inside it.
  • Two roads run backwards. G2 sends results back to the Story to change a claim, and G5 sends a round back into writing. Each is a loop, not a straight line.
  • The Gate is the one road where checks happen. Evidence and rules meet there before you see text.

How a Section runs

0

Open the lab tool

/hai-paper lab opens one session per Section beside its Draft Lab page.

  • First turn: a read-only look at the page, its Story row and its newest Draft.
  • Then the default review round (steps D1 and D2), skipped when the Draft was already reviewed at its version.
  • It replies with one line: reviewed, under 8, pairs, kept, meaning changed, new version.
S

Structure loop AIyou

  • S1 Check the structure: page.py structure <task> prints claims, paragraphs, sentence ranges, words.
  • S2 Compare with the venue's examples of the same article type.
  • S3 A new Draft version; a change to the story comes to you as 2 to 4 versions, and you pick.

◐ Gap: S1 and S2 leave no record, so nothing can show they were done.

D

Draft loop AIyou

  • D1 Rate: fresh reviewers score each paragraph (logic) and each sentence (logic, read, ai); 8 passes.
  • D2 Auto loop: a rewrite for each item under 8; blind A/B keeps only "easier and same meaning"; adopt; rate again.
  • D3 Highlight what the loop cannot settle (claims, story, missing facts) for you.
  • D4 You comment and decide in Draft Lab: cards beside each sentence, Use this, Accept, which writes into Content at once.
  • D5 You check every sentence (✓). A checked sentence gets no new card or ★.

◐ Gap: D3's highlight list has no place on the page yet.

E

Evidence beside both loops youtool

  • Each printed number binds to a value Run: Draft Lab marks a sentence ✓, ❗ bound but not found, or ⚠ a number with no item.
  • Each citation reads name-year; "?" until you verify it; "not in Bib" when the key is missing.
  • Each figure shows its float.pdf, figure and caption as the paper prints them, with its Run under it.
G

The gate before you see text AItool

  • Every rule in must-check.md runs: script checks, then a fresh reviewer for the reading checks.
  • A failure is fixed, or named with its id and reason.

◐ Gap: no single command runs the gate yet (Q5).

C

Close and send AIyou

  • An independent CHECK closes the Section; it never reviews its own writing.
  • Delivery: LaTeX first, then the PDF and a diff against the last round, then Word with tracked changes (a comment only when no edit can answer).
