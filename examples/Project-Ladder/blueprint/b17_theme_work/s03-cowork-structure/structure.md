# Structure of a Work Block (CoWork)

The three trees of this Block type, drawn by `block_structure_page.py` with the same tree as its type's Pattern
(s71 · Studio Type: Block Structure). `> ` lines are a tree: two spaces a level, `name/` a folder, `  # note` a small
word after the name, `  | words` the line's hover. A name that is not fixed, or not agreed yet, is in 〈 〉.

## Folder
> bNN_〈topic〉/  | the Block: one piece of shared work with people outside
>   j00_people/  | always first: who is involved and how to reach them
>     j00_people.md  | the people Job's face
>   jNN_〈request〉/  | a Job: one request or one thing to coordinate; it has no Tasks
>     CHECKLIST.md  | what the request still needs
>     Timeline.md  | what happened, in order
>     jNN_〈request〉.md  | the Job's face: its goal and who has the next move
>   sNN-〈topic〉/  | a Studio topic: a drawing or design to talk through
>   related/  | what came in from outside (papers, notes, files); made when first needed
>   trace/  | a record of each change, by day
>   board.md  | the Block's face: its goal, Jobs and Studio topics

## Job-Task
> 〈Work Block (CoWork)〉
>   〈job-group-name〉  # Job group  | optional: a decade of Job numbers, named on the `bands:` line of board.md
>     j00_people  # Job name  | always the first Job
>     jNN_〈request〉  # Job name  | one request or coordination; no Task level under it
