# Structure of a Work Block (Discovery)

The three trees of this Block type, drawn by `block_structure_page.py` with the same tree as its type's Pattern
(s71 · Studio Type: Block Structure). `> ` lines are a tree: two spaces a level, `name/` a folder, `  # note` a small
word after the name, `  | words` the line's hover. A name that is not fixed, or not agreed yet, is in 〈 〉.

## Folder
> bNN_〈question-area〉/  | the Block: one area of outside evidence to find and read
>   jNN_〈inquiry〉/  | a Job: one inquiry; it is itself the group of its research questions
>     tNN_〈research-question〉/  | a Task: one research question, answered from sources
>       runs/rNN_〈run〉/  | one paper or source, read once
>         result/  | its card, its facts and its one citation entry
>       tNN_〈research-question〉.md  | the Task's face: its goal and state
>     jNN_〈inquiry〉.md  | the Job's face: its goal, close and Tasks in order
>   sNN-〈topic〉/  | a Studio topic: a drawing or design to talk through
>   related/  | what came in from outside (papers, notes, files); made when first needed
>   trace/  | a record of each change, by day
>   board.md  | the Block's face: its goal, Jobs and Studio topics

## Job-Task
> 〈Work Block (Discovery)〉
>   jNN_〈inquiry〉  # Job name  | the Job is the inquiry; there is no Job group above it
>     tNN_〈research-question〉  # Task name  | names its kind on its face (`discovery_type:`)
