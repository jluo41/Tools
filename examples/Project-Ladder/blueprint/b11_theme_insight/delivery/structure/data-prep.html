                    Why these groups and types

                    Each Job is a prep-code version; steps are ordered directly without a Task group.

                    A type names a responsibility from the contract; the files carry no job-type or task-type field.

                    The live Jobs page still groups by number. experiment, source and prepare are step names, not three Task types.
